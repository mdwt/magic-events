IMPORTANT EVENT INFO

REG VENUE & DATE:

Pre-registration: Friday – 26 February

Fernkloof Nature Reserve, Hermanus

• 16:00 pm – 18:00 pm

Saturday – 27 February

Fernkloof Nature Reserve, Hermanus

• 05:00 am – 07:00 am

Parking available opposite the entrance on Contour Street.

Sunday – 28 February

Dutchies, Grotto St, Voelklip, Hermanus

• 06:00 am – 06:30 am

START VENUE:

Day 1: Fernkloof Nature Reserve, Saturday 27 Feb

Day 2: Walker Bay Beach Front, Sunday 28 Feb

START TIMES:

35km: 06:00 | 25km: 7:00am | 15km: 9:00am | 18km: 7:30am

CUT OFF:

Day 1: Final Cut off 2pm

Day 2: Final Cut off 11am

Compulsory KIT :

1. Charged Cell Phone

2. Waterproof jacket

3. Food = energy bars, sandwich, fruit, nuts, chocolate

4. Hydration – Minimum 1 litre to start race!

5. Space Blanket, headlamp, whistle

6. GPS device or ALL Trails App ( The ALL Trails link will be shared)

7. Reusable cup

Our events are Green & Enviro friendly – No Water Sachets, No Paper Cups. Bring your own CUP

DO NOT bring: HEADPHONES, Dogs, Flu or litter.
