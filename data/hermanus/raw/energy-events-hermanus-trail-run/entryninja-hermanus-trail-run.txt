# Hermanus Trail Run

DateFri, 26 Feb

Distances15–53 km

VenueFernkloof, Hermanus

Race details

## About this event

Be ready for a 2-day trail running adventure in the breathtaking coastal town - Hermanus!
The Hermanus Trail Run brings you a weekend of mountain and coastal running, showcasing two of the region’s most iconic landscapes: the Fernkloof Nature Reserve and the pristine Hermanus shoreline. Whether you’re an experienced trail athlete or a weekend adventurer, this event offers something for everyone. 👟🌿

### DAY 1 – Fernkloof Nature Reserve

Experience pure mountain magic as you take on the rugged, scenic trails of Fernkloof. Expect flowing singletrack, technical climbs, panoramic ocean views, and beautiful fynbos-covered terrain.

Distances & Std Entry Fee

- 35km - R1200

- 25km – R1000

- 15km – R650

Both routes offer a proper mountain challenge while still being achievable for runners with moderate trail experience.

### DAY 2 – Walker Bay Beach Run

Day 2 brings a completely different experience — a fast, fun, and refreshing coastal run along the Hermanus shoreline. Enjoy wide open beaches, sea air, and smooth running underfoot.

Distance & Std Entry Fee:

- 18km – R650

This is the perfect complement to the mountain trails of Day 1, giving runners a balanced and unforgettable weekend.

The 18km Beach Run is a linear run.

- Water – Carry at least 1.5L

- Space Blanket

- Re-usable cups

### Why Run Hermanus?

- Two completely different terrain experiences

- World-class views of mountains, coastline, and fynbos

- A well-organised trail weekend suitable for various fitness levels

- Perfect summer trail getaway in one of SA’s most beautiful towns

Join us for an unforgettable trail experience in Hermanus — where mountains meet the ocean!
