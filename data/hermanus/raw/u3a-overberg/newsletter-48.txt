Adventures of a tunnel rat [Part 2]

by Hennie Greef

The people, machines and challenges behind underground construction

Hennie Greeff once before talked about his experience as a “Tunnel Rat” but that was largely a travelogue. In this presentation, he will expand on the technology and techniques of building tunnels with some case studies.

CHILDREN’S CHRISTMAS PRESENTS

FRIDAY 27 NOVEMBER 2026

At our final U3A lecture of the year, Letitia Snyman will present ‘The Story of Teddy Bears’.

It is a fitting occasion to invite members once again to bring Christmas presents for children in need. The Hermanus Rotary Club will distribute the gifts through early childhood development initiatives.

Please choose gifts suitable for children aged 3 to 6 eg:

- Small items of clothing

- Stationery such as pencils and crayons

- Picture books, sports equipment and similar items are all welcome

Please avoid food and sweets!

Wrap each gift and mark it for a girl, a boy or either.

Please indicate the suitable age where relevant.

We suggest a limit of R150 per gift so that the presents are broadly of similar value and to avoid a big difference between presents…. If you’d like to spend more, please consider bringing an extra gift.

Last year’s response was heartening.

Thank you for helping us bring some Christmas cheer to the children again this year.

Gert Claassen

FRIDAY 27 NOVEMBER at 10 am in the Catholic Church Hall

The story of Teddy Bears

by Letitia Snyman

Theodore (Teddy) Roosevelt was the 26th president of the United States, serving from 1901 to 1909. He was a very popular and effective president, often called the “first modern president” because of his extension of the power of the Executive during his two terms in office. He also involved himself and his country in international affairs and was the first American to win the Nobel Peace prize in 1905 for his role in negotiating an end to the war between Russia and Japan.

In this presentation, we will also indulge in looking at another, more endearing and charming aspect of his legacy – the Teddy Bear! We invite you to share in the fun of looking at the origin and evolution of the phenomenon that is the stuffed animal toy that bears his name!

U3A MEMBERSHIP FEES FOR 2027 !

The U3A membership fee has remained unchanged for more than ten years. Throughout this period, the Management Committee has been very conscious of managing our finances judiciously and keeping costs to a minimum.

We do not pay speakers a fee or honorarium, although we reimburse travel and accommodation costs where necessary. The Committee has funded all our audio-visual equipment, including periodic upgrades, from U3A resources, and the equipment is insured.

We also pay rental for the hall and endeavour to recover as much of this cost as possible from the door fee.

We remunerate our technician, who provides an essential and consistently outstanding service.

However, costs continue to increase.

In addition, there has been some unusual capital expenditure during the past two years. This has resulted in an operating deficit and has required us to draw on our capital reserves.

After careful consideration, the Committee has therefore decided to introduce a modest increase in our fees for 2027.

The annual membership fee will increase from R150 to R180 per person, while the door fee for visitors will increase from R25 to R30.

The door fee for members will remain unchanged at R10.

U3A offers approximately 86 lectures each year, and the Committee continually strives to engage quality speakers covering a wide range of subjects. We trust that members will agree that, even with these modest increases, U3A continues to offer excellent value.

We thank you for your loyal and continued support and look forward to another year of interesting, informative and stimulating lectures in 2027.

Gert Claassen
Chairman
