## About this event

## Welcome to Titan Trail Running

Let’s Hit the Trails

If you love running, a bit of adventure, and the kind of routes that make your legs work but leave you smiling at the finish, you’re in the right place.

Titan Trail Running is about well-built routes, great locations, and a community of runners who enjoy being out there. Whether you’re chasing a podium or just keen for a solid morning on the trails, there’s a place for you on our start line.

And yes, walking is allowed on all distances. Run it, hike it, race it. Just get it done.

## What You Can Expect

### Proper Trails

Each event features carefully mapped routes designed to be interesting, runnable, and rewarding. You can also expect water stations along the route to keep you hydrated.

### Accurate Timing

Your race, your result. We time every event so you can track your performance and see where you stack up [ and get your Vitality points ;) ]

### Podiums & Medals

If you’re fast, we’ll celebrate you properly. If you’re not, you still earn your medal, and your bragging rights.

### Post-Run Atmosphere

Titan events are designed to be more than a start and finish line. Expect:

- Great coffee

- Good food

- Wine at selected venues

- Space to sit, relax, and enjoy the vibe

We want you to stick around after you’ve crossed the line!

### Professional Support

Medical teams are on standby at every event, and our routes are clearly marked and well managed so you can focus on running.

### Cool Partners

We have some really cool partners we invite to display their awesome product on the day. They are there for you to make your running experience amazing.

For a cool discount on the best electrolytes on the market, use code: Titan20 here -> sci_coreSALT to be race ready on the day!

### Good Vibes Only

No egos. No pressure. Just people who enjoy being active, being outdoors, and being part of something well organised.

## Our Venues

Titan Trail Running takes place at standout locations across the Western Cape.

Each event location brings its own flavour, but the Titan standard stays the same: solid routes, great scenery, and a welcoming atmosphere.

## Distances & Format

We offer accessible distances:

- 5KM - Start at 07:45

- 10KM - Start at 07:30

Wave starts keep things flowing, with early morning kick-offs to make the most of the day.

### Registration

Registration open at 06:30, make sure to come early to collect you timing band at the registration booth (look out for the TITAN gazebo). You only need your full name to register.

So what do you say?

Sign up. Show up. Bring your people.

Let’s hit the trails together.
