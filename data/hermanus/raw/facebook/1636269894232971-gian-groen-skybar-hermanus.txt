An evening of exceptional music awaits at Skybar, Hermanus, when acclaimed singer-songwriter Gian Groen takes to the stage for an intimate unplugged performance on Thursday, 10 December.
With only his songs, stories and acoustic sound, Gian will share the music that has earned him a place among South Africa's most respected Afrikaans songwriters.
Audiences can expect an authentic performance filled with heartfelt lyrics, memorable melodies and the personal stories behind the songs.
Paired with delicious food, great company and the welcoming atmosphere of Skybar, this promises to be a memorable night.
Book now and experience Gian Groen – up close, unplugged and unforgettable.
