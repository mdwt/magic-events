Dust off your fedora: Indiana Jones is coming to Landskein.
Saturday 28 November
Gates open at 17:00 · Movie starts at 19:30
We're screening Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981) in the amphitheatre, under the fairy lights and the Stanford sky.
Come early, find your spot and settle in with a glass of Landskein wine, a wood-fired pizza and some popcorn from the bar.
Bring blankets, cushions or camping chairs, and a warm layer for when the sun goes down. Outside food and drinks aren't allowed. If the weather turns, we'll let ticket holders know by email.
