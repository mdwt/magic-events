Ever wondered what it takes to create the perfect wine blend?
Join us for The Blending Table, a new hands-on wine experience at Benguela Cove Lagoon Wine Estate, where you'll step into the role of winemaker for the morning.
Taste four to six component wines from the current vintage and discover how each one contributes to the final blend. Guided by our winemaker, you'll experiment with different ratios, refine your creation, and craft a wine that reflects your personal taste.
Once you've perfected your blend, you'll compare it with the winemaker's own selection before bottling and labelling your wine to take home.
Every guest leaves with a bottle of their own creation.
What's Included
Guided wine blending session with the winemaker
Tasting of 4 to 6 component wines
Tasting notes and precision blending equipment
Bottling and labelling of your wine
A personalised bottle to take home
