Andrew Young Live at Benguela Cove: an afternoon of live music beside the lagoon, with an evening still to come.
Saturday, 9 January 2027 Doors 16:30 | Show 17:30 Benguela Cove Lagoon Wine Estate, R43, Walker Bay, Hermanus
The Saxophone Journal USA has called Andrew Young "one of the most complete saxophone players in the world today." Expect Beatles classics, iconic movie themes, soul, rhythm and blues and rock, all transformed by his distinctive musicianship, humour and warmth. You do not need to love jazz to enjoy this show, you only need to enjoy good live music beside the water.
Make a full afternoon of it. Moody Lagoon Restaurant serves à la carte lunch from 12:00 to 17:00 on the day, and gourmet picnic baskets are available on the lawns from 11:00 to 16:00. On-site villa accommodation is also available if you would like to stay the night.
