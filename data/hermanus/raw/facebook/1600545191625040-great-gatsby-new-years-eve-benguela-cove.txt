Step onto the shores of the Bot River Lagoon and into the Roaring Twenties. This New Year's Eve, Benguela Cove transforms into a Great Gatsby scene for one night only, live DJ, premium sound and lighting, an unlimited 360-degree video booth, and a midnight countdown into 2027.
Dress the part: think flapper dresses, feathers and black tie glamour.
What's included?
Standard Ticket, from R850pp (early bird until 15 November): entry, welcome drink, DJ and sound, lighting, video booth, parking, midnight countdown and Gatsby-themed décor
Picnic Basket Ticket, from R1,350pp: everything above, plus a picnic to enjoy in the New Year's Eve Area
Children's Ticket (under 12), R450: soft drinks and age-appropriate entertainment
Only 180 guests, so early booking is recommended.
Want to make a full night of it? Book a table at Moody Lagoon Restaurant beforehand, or stay over in one of the estate's Waterfront Villas and skip the drive home.
