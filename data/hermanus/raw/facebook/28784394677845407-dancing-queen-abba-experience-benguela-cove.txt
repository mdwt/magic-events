After a completely sold-out performance in 2025, Mike & The Harmonix return to Benguela Cove with their spectacular tribute show, Dancing Queen: The Ultimate ABBA Experience.
Join us as our courtyard is transformed into a dazzling Greek island paradise for an unforgettable evening celebrating the timeless music of ABBA.
Sing along to all your favourites, including Dancing Queen, Mamma Mia, Waterloo, Gimme! Gimme! Gimme! and more, brought to life with incredible live vocals, authentic costumes, stunning staging, and all the glittering energy of the world's favourite pop group.
And when the final curtain falls, the party continues with a post-show disco under the stars.
Two dates: 7 November 2026 • 30 January 2027
Doors Open: 16:30
Make a day of it Pre-book a picnic to collect on arrival, or enjoy an à la carte meal at Moody Lagoon Restaurant earlier in the day before the show.
Ticket Prices • Adults: R500 • Children (5-17 years): R325 • Family Bundle (2 Adults + 2 Children under 17): R1,450
Dust off your flares, gather your Dancing Queens and Super Troupers, and get ready for a night of nostalgia, fun, and unforgettable entertainment at Benguela Cove.
