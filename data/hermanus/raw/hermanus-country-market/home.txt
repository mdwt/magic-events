## Experience the Magic of the Hermanus Country Market

Discover the vibrant charm of the Hermanus Country Market, where community, creativity, and connection come together every Saturday from 9 AM to 2 PM.

🌿 Handmade | Homegrown | Local – From fresh produce and handcrafted goods to artisan foods and unique gifts, every stall tells a story of passion and craftsmanship.

🐶 Pet-Friendly & Family-Friendly – Bring the whole family (furry friends included!) to enjoy a lively, welcoming atmosphere. Kids will love the Kiddies’ Corner, jungle gym, and open play spaces.

🎶 A Feast for the Senses – Enjoy great music, gourmet treats, fresh flowers, lush plants, and stunning artisanal creations in a bustling, open-air setting.

👩‍🎨 Supporting Local Entrepreneurs – With over 66% of our stalls run by female-owned businesses, shopping at the market means supporting dreams and small businesses.

Join us for a fun-filled Saturday morning and experience the heart and soul of Hermanus – one market visit at a time!

## Visiting the Market

We are open every Saturday, year round, from 9am to 2pm.

You will find us at the Hermanus Cricket Club at the corner of Fairways Avenue & Jose Burman Drive, next to the Generations School. There’s plenty of parking available.

In December & January we also hold our popular Summer Night Markets from 5pm to 9pm on Wednesday evenings.

## Spring Vibes, Fynbos Blooms & Whale Season at the Market.

As warmer days and fresh breezes sweep across the Overberg, spring brings Hermanus to life in full, vibrant colour! September is one of the most magical times in our coastal town — perfect for sunny Saturday mornings spent exploring our incredible selection of locally made spring crafts, fashion, and botanical gifts, and sharing fresh food, great beverages, and wonderful company under the trees.

Whale Season in Full Swing & Spring Energy. Walker Bay is bustling as the majestic Southern Right Whales breach and frolic along our coastline! Make a full day of it: stroll the cliff paths, head over to the market to fuel up with artisanal coffee, delicious brunch options, gourmet eats, craft beers, and explore our incredible selection of locally made spring crafts, fashion, and botanical gifts.

Hermanus Flower Show & Blooming Fynbos | 24 – 27 September. Spring is the ultimate time to explore our world-famous floral kingdom. The Hermanus Botanical Society Flower Show takes place from 24 to 27 September 2026 at the nearby Fernkloof Nature Reserve, where the fynbos bursts into full bloom and magnificent proteas paint the mountain slopes. Pair your botanical excursion with a stop at the market to celebrate the beauty of the Overberg!

Heritage Weekend & School Holidays | 23 Sept – 5 Oct. With public schools breaking up for the third-term holidays from Thursday, 24 September (Heritage Day) through Monday, 5 October, the Hermanus Country Market is an absolute must-visit for families and holidaymakers. Bring the kids for fun activities, enjoy the live music, and savour the best long-weekend atmosphere in town.

## Our Traders

We have over 60 traders showcasing a wonderful selection of locally-produced goods, from fresh farm vegetables and artisan breads to delicious prepared foods and pastries. You’ll also find a variety of Artists, Artisans, Handmade Apparel & Accessories, Jewellery & Cosmetics, and Hand-crafted items, making our market the perfect place to find unique souvenirs or gifts for loved ones.

Hermanus Country Market is not simply a place to shop; it’s a hub of female entrepreneurship. Over 66% of our stallholders are women-owned and managed, and therefore by shopping at our market, you are directly investing in the dreams and aspirations of countless women entrepreneurs. You can read more about our “Celebrating Women” Campaign which kicked off in August 2024.
