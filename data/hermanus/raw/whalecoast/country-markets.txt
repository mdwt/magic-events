COUNTRY MARKETS

Country Markets:

Saturday Morning Market

Stanford boasts its own unique Saturday Morning Market along Queen Victoria Street from 09h00–12h00.

Fresh produce from local farmers and local crafts are offered, as well as delicious homemade and home-baked goodies made from the heart! There are also old treasures waiting to be found and given a new lease on life.

Stanford Sunset Market

Stanford is the place to be on the last Friday of every month from 18h00–20h00 at the Old Village Green, except during June, July and August.

This market is reminiscent of the old days when farmers and traders used to gather and display their goods for sale on market day. Visit the market for exceptional homemade food, live music and real village hospitality, where locals and visitors mingle after the sun has long set.
