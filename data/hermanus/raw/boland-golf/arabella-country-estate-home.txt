## Explore Arabella Country Estate

Welcome to Arabella Country Estate. Nestled on the banks of the Bot River Lagoon, at the heart of the Kogelberg Biosphere, lies an unobtrusive
18-hole vista for the astute golfer. A place where the community lives inspired, and visitors find pristine tranquillity. But most of all, a place where we promote growth while sustaining nature.

## Living

Arabella Country Estate is the epitome of luxury real estate in the Kogelberg Biosphere. Our community comes from all walks of life, but have one thing in common – inspired living. The Arabella Country Estate lifestyle enables a higher level of self-actualisation.

## Golf

Designed and built by the highly acclaimed Peter Matkovich, the Arabella Golf Course seamlessly integrates with nature. Here golfers can pursue our scenic 18-hole, par 72 championship course edged by more than 1 600 species of wild Fynbos. Enjoy the view!

## Hotel, Golf & Spa

Get an exclusive peek at pristine Kogelberg tranquillity. Visitors to the Arabella Hotel, Golf & Spa have a unique opportunity to experience a perfect blend of luxury and rare natural beauty. Complete your rejuvenating escape at any of our three restaurants.
