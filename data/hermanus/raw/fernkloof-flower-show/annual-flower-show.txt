Annual Flower Show

Hermanus Botanical Society Flower Show

24 - 27 September 2026

Fernkloof Nature Reserve

RULES FOR RAFFLE

Tickets to be sold by HBS members and others who are friends of Fernkloof Nature Reserve

To be valid and included in the draw, ticket counterfoil must be official, printed ticket counterfoil showing HBS registration number with NLCSA, ticket number and name and contact telephone number of the purchaser

No ticket may be sold to a minor under the age of 18 years

All tickets will be sold for the price of R20 per ticket and all valid tickets sold will be in the draw

Ticket sales will end at 12md on Sunday 27 September

The draw will be done by the Chairperson of HBS, assisted by a Society member at 12md on Sunday 27 September 2026 in Fernkloof Nature Reserve and will be witnessed by an independent person

The draw for the prizes will be done in alphabetical order. The winning ticket number, name and telephone number of purchaser of the winning tickets and details of the seller of the winnning tickets, will be recorded

The award of prizes to the holder of winning tickets will be final once the draw has been concluded

Prize winners present at the draw will be handed their prize. All prizewinners not present at the draw will be contacted personally by the Raffle promoter or assistant within 24 hours and arrangements made for the winners to claim their prizes before 1 December 2026 at the latest.

In the event that a prize winner cannot be contacted before the expiry date of the prize they have won or if a prize has not been claimed by 1 December, the prize will be returned to the donor

RULES FOR SILENT AUCTION

TIME: SATURDAY 26 SEPTEMBER 2026 10am - 12md

Every bid is a binding contract. No refunds or exchanges. Inspect the piece before bidding

Minimum increments apply and bids must increase by the stated minimum amount; lower bids are invalid

As-is condition: artwork is sold in its current state

The next highest bid wins

Closing time: bidding closes at exactly 3pm (15:00)

No bids are accepted after the current announcement

Legible writing: unreadable names or bid ammounts will be disqualified

Item pick-up: winners must pay in full and take the artwork on 26 September by 4pm ( 16:00)
