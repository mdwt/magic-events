## Whale Watching

# TOUR OVERVIEW

LETS GO! The whale watching experience begins with an on-land briefing led by our whale specialist guide – giving you some more information on the day’s plans ahead.

We then set off from the Hermanus New Harbour in search of the ‘gentle giants.’

During the tour, the friendly, knowledgeable crew are on hand to add that personal touch allowing for an all-round great experience.

With all eyes on watch, the excitement builds for who can see the first whale of the trip!

While around the whales, they are in charge – as we enter their world – getting to spend some unforgettable moments in nature.

Our onboard team, will help interpret the whales (and other marine life encountered), keeping you informed, providing insight into the behaviours of the whales while we watch them.

Our onboard videographer helps capture these moments, allowing you to really just be in the moment & absorb the experience.

On return to land, peruse the local craft and souvenirs available, order your USB movie of the tour which is ready upon return or simply relax at our outdoor seating on the edge of the quay with reading material on all things whales.

When is Hermanus Whale Season?

June to Mid-December Annually

The peak Hermanus whale watching season typically runs from June to end November. This is the time when the southern right whales migrate to the coastal waters of Hermanus to mate, calve, and nurse their young. Visitors to Hermanus during this period can witness these magnificent creatures in their natural habitat, breaching, spouting, and tail-slapping.

The town of Hermanus is known as one of the best whale watching destinations in the world. The peak whale season runs from June to end November, annually.

-->

What could you encounter?

Southern Right, Humpback & Brydes whales

During whale season, Hermanus is lucky to the host the bulk of the Southern Right Whales that migrate to the South African coastline. In addition the Humpback and Brydes whales can be seen.

We focus the tour on searching and spending time with the whales but the marine life in the bay is diverse and there is a good chance of encountering Cape fur seals, various species of dolphins and an abundance of sea birds including the African penguin.

Whale Species: Southern Right (Jun-Dec), Humpback (June-September) & Bryde’s whales (all year)

Even with our success rate of 99% for whale & dolphin sighting, it is nature and we cannot ‘guarantee’ sightings. If on the rare occasion no whales or dolphins (i.e. cetaceans) are encountered, we offer a 50% refund on your ticket price paid or the opportunity to join us on the next available tour, for free. WHAT SHOULD YOU BRING?

A windbreaker, camera & more

In Hermanus, the weather is notoriously fickle, especially during the peak season which falls within South African spring. Temperatures can soar to 30 degrees Celsius or higher, but on other days, mist, rain, or wind can make conditions feel much colder.

It’s important to remember that layering is key when dressing for unpredictable weather. So, be sure to pack appropriately and enjoy your adventure on the high seas!
