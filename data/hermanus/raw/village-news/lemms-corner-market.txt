# Lemm’s Corner Market

28 November.09:00 -14:00

Free

- « Benguela Cove parkrun

- Rosoberry Harvest Fest 2026 »

Every Saturday. Antiques, vintage and retro items, certified organic vegetables, flowers, candles, pottery, jewellery and more. Also open during the First Fridays Art Walk from 16:00 until dark. Weather dependent. Stalls available for bakers and creatives: WhatsApp Elmine on 082 854 7491.

WhereLemm’s Corner, Hermanus

CostFree
