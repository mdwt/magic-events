# Focker-In-Law Sneak Peak Premiere screening at Road House Cinema Whale Coast

26 November.20:00 -23:00

R120.00

- « Hermanus Chess Club

- Bhuki Café »

Get ready for a night of laughs, fun and a little family chaos! Join us at Road House Cinema Whale Coast on 26 November for an exclusive sneak peek screening of Focker-In-Law.

Bring your partner, friends or even the in-laws and enjoy welcome drinks on arrival, courtesy of Walker Bay Estate, home to Birkenhead Brewery, plus loads of exciting prizes up for grabs!

Arrive from 6:30 PM to enjoy a drink, mingle and soak up the atmosphere before the screening kicks off at 8:00 PM. You won’t want to miss this one!

WhereShop G57, Whale Coast Mall, R43, Sandbaai, Hermanus

CostR120.00

Phone028 312 3189

Emailinfo_wct@roadhousecinema.co.za

Bookingsroadhousecinema.co.za
