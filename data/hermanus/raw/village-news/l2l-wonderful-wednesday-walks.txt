# L2L Wonderful Wednesday Walks

25 November.06:30

R20 donation

- « Whalers Social Run

- Stimulus Coffee and Conversation Group »

Every Wednesday. Families, including well-behaved dogs on leads, are invited to join the 5km-in-an-hour walk. R20 donation (or more if you wish). Check the Lighthouse2Lighthouse Ladies Facebook or Instagram pages for the starting point. For the L2L walk alternating between Franskraal, Kleinbaai and De Kelders, phone Betsie at 083 395 7565.

WhereHermanus Waterfront

CostR20 donation
