# Nadine Live in Hermanus

5 December.19:00 -21:00

R230 per ticket, children under 10 is a R100 per ticket

- « Panthera Africa Sunset Visits

- Landskein Cheese & Wine Market »

Music with purpose! Beloved South African singer and television presenter Nádine officially celebrated her 30-year milestone in the music industry.

She received the prestigious 30 Year Contribution Honorary Award at the Afri-Indie Music Awards. All proceeds raised on the evening will be gifted to Camphill Farm Community Hermanus.

WhereCurro Hermanus, Sandbaai

CostR230 per ticket, children under 10 is a R100 per ticket

ContactMichelle

Phone0825653627

Emailmichellevz@camphill-hermanus.org.za
