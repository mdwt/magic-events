# Alan Committie: AV-A-LAUGH-TA

28 November.19:30 -21:00

R180.00

- « Panthera Africa Sunset Visits

- Donkey Sanctuary: free tours »

It’s been a year, right?

If you, like me, could really do with some belly laughs to slide into 2027 on the right foot, look no further than Alan Committie’s brand-new show, which goes curtains-up at Road House Cinema in Whale Coast Mall.

Alan Committie is blasting into the festive season with A-VA-LAUGH-TA. True to form, audiences can expect a high-energy, stand-up special designed to banish the blues with interstellar wordplay, physical comedy, and Committie’s trademark take-downs of the crowd, as this award-winning comic tries to make sense of a year where everything feels a bit… alien.

WhereShop G57, Whale Coast Mall, R43, Sandbaai, Hermanus

CostR180.00

Phone021 109 3770

Emailinfo_wct@roadhousecinema.co.za

Bookingsroadhousecinema.co.za
