# The Metropolitan Opera: Carmen (Bizet) Encore at Road House Cinema Whale Coast

23 November.12:30 -16:00

R175.00

- « Donkey Sanctuary: free tours

- Hermanus Hacking Group »

EXPERIENCE CARMEN ON THE BIG SCREEN!

An unforgettable tale of passion, obsession and betrayal awaits! Experience Bizet’s iconic opera, Carmen, in this spectacular Met Opera production, featuring mezzo-soprano Clémentine Margaine and tenor Roberto Alagna.

Road House Cinema Whale Coast

23 November – ONE DAY ONLY!

Two shows: 12:30 PM & 6:00 PM

Two showtimes. One extraordinary opera. Don’t miss your chance to experience the magic of the Met Opera on the big screen!

Book your tickets now!

WhereShop G57, Whale Coast Mall, R43, Sandbaai, Hermanus

CostR175.00

Phone028 312 3189
