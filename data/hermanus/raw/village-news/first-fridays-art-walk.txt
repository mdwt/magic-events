# First Fridays Art Walk

4 December.17:00 -20:00

Free

- « Hermanus Duplicate Bridge Club

- Creedence – A Clearwater Revival Tribute at Hermanus Golf Club »

Every first Friday of the month. Join the 23 art galleries in the Hermanus CBD for an evening art walk, every first Friday of the month, when all the galleries stay open late. Maps at all participating galleries: paintings, sculptures, ceramics, jewellery and photography.

WhereHermanus CBD

CostFree
