# The 4th Annual Most Influential Icons Gala Dinner

4 December.17:00 -21:30

R370

- « Hermanus Duplicate Bridge Club

- Creedence – A Clearwater Revival Tribute at Hermanus Golf Club »

Most Influential Icons Gala (MIIGD) is an annual celebration that honours individuals and organisations whose leadership, service, innovation, and dedication have made a meaningful impact within their communities and beyond. The gala recognises icons from diverse sectors, including business, education, arts and culture, community development, youth empowerment, sports, health, and public service.

More than an awards event, the Most Influential Icons Gala is a platform for inspiration, networking, and collaboration. Through keynote addresses, powerful performances, and the recognition of outstanding changemakers, the evening encourages others to lead with purpose.

WhereMunicipal Auditorium, Hermanus

CostR370
