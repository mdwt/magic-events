# Sourdough Baking Class

26 November.11:00 -15:00

R450

- « Stimulus Coffee and Conversation Group

- Al-Anon Meeting »

Every Thursday. Join Biga Bakery’s hands-on class and discover the secrets to creating artisan dough, guided by expert bakers. Take home your own bread, a live starter plant and all the ingredients you need. Freshly baked quiche and coffee included. Only five spots at R450pp. Now also on Saturdays at 09:00.

WhereBiga Bakery, 12 Village Lane, Hemel-en-Aarde, Sandbaai

CostR450

Phone028 125 0188
