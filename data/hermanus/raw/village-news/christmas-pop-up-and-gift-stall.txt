# Christmas Pop-up and Gift Stall

28 November.09:00 -14:00

Free

- « Benguela Cove parkrun

- Rosoberry Harvest Fest 2026 »

We are hosting a Christmas Pop-Up & Gift Stall at the church on the 28th November, 2026.

Selling a variety of delicious treats, proudly showing of our exhibition of exquisite needlework, woodwork, and arts and craft, and offer a quiet reading corner where you are invited to join other book lovers. The morning include a “Cake Bake-off” competition, and everyone is welcome to participate.

We kindly extend a warm invitation to our community and visitors to join us for a Celebration of delicious treats, creativity, craftsmanship and community fun.

WhereThe Anglican Chapel of St Francis of Assisi, Betty's Bay

CostFree
