# The Cat in the Hat (3D) – Pre-release screening at Whale Coast Mall

28 November.13:00 -15:00

3D Adults - R129.00 · 3D Kids (under 10) -R109.00 · 3D Seniors - R109.00

BE THE FIRST TO SEE THE CAT IN THE HAT!

One day only! Join us at Road House Cinema Whale Coast Mall on 28 November for an exclusive 3D pre-release screening of The Cat in the Hat!

Miss it, and you’ll have to wait with the rest of the world until the official release on 4 December.

Don’t wait around – book your tickets now and be first in line for the fun!

ONE DAY ONLY. ONE SHOW ONLY. ONE VERY MISCHIEVOUS CAT.

WhereShop G57, Whale Coast Mall, R43, Sandbaai, Hermanus

Cost3D Adults - R129.00 | 3D Kids (under 10) -R109.00 | 3D Seniors - R109.00

Phone028 312 3189

Emailinfo_wct@roadhousecinema.co.za
