Local is Lekker @ The Fabled Table, La Vierge Hermanus

Hemel-en-Aarde Valley Rd

Saturday 5 December · 12:00 – 17:00

Kiddies welcome! Bring the whole family.

Spend a laid-back afternoon among our vineyards on the Hemel-en-Aarde Ridge with freshly made pizza, delicious wine and live music by Edian Barnard. There’s plenty of lawn for little ones to run around while you relax.

Your R500 ticket for two includes:

• Two pizzas of your choice

• One bottle from our Story Teller range

• Live music by Edian Barnard

• Entry for two guests

Only 50 tickets available. Swipe through, then book your spot before they’re gone.

WhereHemel-en-Aarde, R320, Valley, Road, Hermanus, 7200

CostR500 for two people, kiddies under 12 are free
