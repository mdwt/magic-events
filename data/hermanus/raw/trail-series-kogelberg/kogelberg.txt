# Kogelberg Biosphere

##### Sun, 16 Aug '26

Experience some of the most spectacular routes in the TSSL, through the Kogelberg Biosphere Reserve surrounding Kleinmond.

This is as diverse a route as you can get with coastal pathways, forests and mountain trails complete with spectacular ocean views.

Registration: 06:30-8:00am at the start/finish
