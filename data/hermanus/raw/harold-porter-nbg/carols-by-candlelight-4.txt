# Carols by candlelight

-

10/11/2026

Date: 17/12/2026

Time: 7:30 pm - 10:15 pm

Location: Concert area

Harold Porter

We are thrilled to announce that our Carols by Candlelight event will be presented by the Hermanus Rotary Club! Join us with your friends and family, bring along your picnic basket, and enjoy singing your favorite Christmas carols.

Tickets can be purchased online via Webtickets or at the Harold Porter Entrance office.

Gates open at 18:30, with music Background Christmas music will be played from 18:30 to 19:15.

The concert will start at 19:30 with the Nativity Pageant, followed by the Christmas Carol performances and saxophonist.

Adults: R160

Youth (age 6 to 18 ): R65

Children 0 to 6 years: free.

### ABOUT SANBI

The South African National Biodiversity Institute (SANBI) contributes to South Africa’s sustainable development by facilitating access to biodiversity data, generating information and knowledge, building capacity, providing policy advice, showcasing and conserving biodiversity in its national botanical and zoological gardens.
