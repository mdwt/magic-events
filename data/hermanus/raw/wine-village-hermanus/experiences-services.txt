##
We Offer

###
Wine Tastings

### Daily Wine Tastings

Enjoy complimentary wine tastings every day. Our tasting selection changes regularly, allowing us to showcase a variety of wines, including whites, reds, Cap Classique, and other exciting discoveries from our shelves.

These informal tastings are designed to help you explore new wines, learn more about different producers and styles, and discover something you might not have considered before. Feel free to browse the store at your own pace while enjoying a glass—there is no charge to participate.

### Private Wine Tastings

Looking for a more personalised experience? We offer private wine tastings by prior arrangement for corporate events, celebrations, client entertainment, or groups wanting a guided tasting experience.

Simply let us know your requirements, and our team will work with you to create a tasting tailored to your preferences. Pricing is determined by the size of the group, the wines selected, and the level of presentation required.

###
Weekend Wine Experiences

### Winery Counter Takeovers

Join us on most Saturdays as we welcome a featured winery to Wine Village for an informal tasting experience.

For a small tasting fee (typically R50 per person), you'll have the opportunity to sample the wines being showcased and chat directly with a representative from the winery. It's a relaxed and enjoyable way to discover new wines, learn the stories behind the labels, and ask questions about the wines you're tasting.

Our Counter Takeovers are usually held from 12:00 pm to 3:00 pm, and no booking is required—simply pop in and join us.

Occasionally, we host larger or specialised events with different pricing, venues, or times. Any changes will always be clearly communicated on the event flyer.

Join our WhatsApp group (below) or follow our social media pages to stay up to date with upcoming tastings, featured wineries, and special events.

###
Personal Monthly Curations

Sign up for our newsletters to discover something new every month with a wine selection curated by JP Rossouw.

You are also most welcome to chat to us about your own monthly case of wine. Whether you're looking to explore different varieties, build your wine knowledge, enjoy trusted favourites, or simply leave the choosing to us, our team can create a monthly selection tailored to your tastes.

Tell us your preferences—such as your favourite wine styles, price range, or any producers you'd like to explore—and we'll hand-pick a collection for you each month.

Every curation is unique, making it an enjoyable way to discover new wines while knowing each bottle has been carefully selected with your preferences in mind.

Contact us by email to start your personalised monthly wine journey.

###
International and Local Shipping

Whether you're sending a gift across South Africa or shipping your favourite South African wines overseas, our team is here to help.

#### Local Shipping

We offer nationwide courier delivery throughout South Africa.

- Courier rates start from R250.

- Complimentary shipping is available on orders over R2,500 (within
qualifying delivery areas).

#### International Shipping

We ship to a wide range of international destinations, subject to country-specific regulations and courier availability. If you're interested in an international shipment, simply contact us and we'll guide you through the process, provide a shipping quotation, and assist with all the necessary arrangements.

For international orders:

- No South African VAT is charged on exported wines, meaning the 15% VAT is removed from your invoice.

- Shipping costs are calculated according to the value of your order. A final shipping cost can only be confirmed once your invoice has been prepared.

- Delivery typically takes 21–30 business days, depending on the destination and customs clearance.

#### Additional Shipping Charges

The following surcharges apply to international shipments:

- R30 per bottle of sparkling wine.

- R50 per bottle of spirits (spirits cannot be shipped to the USA).

- US$65 per six bottles for shipments to Alaska and Hawaii.

- Magnum bottles, larger formats, and wooden presentation boxes may incur
additional shipping charges.

#### Shipping Restrictions

Please note the following restrictions:

- Shipments to Singapore and Japan are limited to 12 bottles per consignee and
delivery address.

- Unfortunately, we cannot ship to the following destinations:
Northern Ireland; Utah; Mississippi; Alabama; Delaware; Rhode Island; Kentucky;
Arkansas; Oklahoma; Channel Islands

#### Travelling with Wine?

If you're taking your wines home yourself, we also stock 6-bottle and 12-bottle Styrofoam shipping boxes, as well as single-bottle protective sleeves.

Many travellers find this to be a cost-effective alternative, as you'll typically only pay your airline's additional baggage or excess weight fee.
