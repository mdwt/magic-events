## ABOUT

BACK BY POPULAR DEMAND — ANDREW YOUNG LIVE AT HERMANUS GOLF CLUB

For the 8th consecutive year, international saxophonist Andrew Young returns to the Hermanus Golf Club, a firm favourite on his Coast to Coast Tour.

With his unmistakable saxophone sound, infectious energy and warm humour, Andrew brings an evening of rock, pop and soul favourites to this beautiful setting. Expect timeless melodies, toe-tapping rhythms and songs that bring memories flooding back.

Whether you’ve joined us year after year or are discovering Andrew for the first time, gather your friends and enjoy the music, atmosphere and magic that keep audiences coming back.

Seven years of wonderful memories — let’s make some more!

Book your tickets at www.andrewyoung.net or QUICKET
