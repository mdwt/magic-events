## ABOUT

Hermanus PRIDE is celebrating its annual Pride party at the most iconic place, at FICKS Pool Restaurant in Hermanus.

We will celebrate all day, beginning with our PRIDE market at the ONLY SPECIALS LIFESTYLE HUB in the morning, followed by a PRIDE Fun Walk in the afternoon from there through town to FICKS.

The market and walk are free of charge, the ticket price is for the party at FICKS.
