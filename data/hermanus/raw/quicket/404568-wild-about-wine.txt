## ABOUT

Lomond Summer Events

Our Lomond Summer Events are back for the 2026 festive season and run into our Blessing of the Harvest events in January 2027 during harvest time.

A nature-inspired, end of year tasting.

Escape to the wild beauty of our farm for a summer celebration beside our beloved farm dam. Surrounded by the fynbos and fresh air of Cape Agulhas, discover wines shaped by this extraordinary landscape. Raise a glass to the year gone by, good company and the wild spirit of great wine.

Join us at the Tasting Room at 11:30am and we will take you across our dam by boat, past our cellar and to our picnic site.

Here you will enjoy a wine tasting hosted by Anné van Heerden with our Harvest Table lunch set out next to the dam in true Cape Agulhas style.

Alternative venues on the farm will be used if the weather doesn’t permit use of our picnic site.

Cost: R500 per person includes the wine tasting and our substantial Harvest Table for lunch

Time: Arrive at 11h30 at the Tasting Room, thereafter you’ll be transported to the picnic site (or alternative venue if the weather doesn’t play along).

The event will end by 15h00, but transport will be available in the event that you have to leave earlier.

What to bring: Sun hat and sunscreen, windproof jacket, slip off shoes for easy access to the boat. The picnic site is shaded with seating, has ablution facilities and Wi-Fi.

Fastest Fingers First as seats are limited.

Our Lomond Summer Events are always thoroughly enjoyed by our enthusiastic, wine-loving Cellar Club Members. We really hope you can join us this year.

Tel: 028 388 0095

Email: info@lomond.co.za

WhatsApp: 082 908 0098
