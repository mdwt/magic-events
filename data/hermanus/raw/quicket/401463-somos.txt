## ABOUT

Somos presents: Sunset House & Dining

Join us on the terrace for an 7hour debut day-to-night journey curated by Somos. We are bringing together good food, great energy, and house music as day turns into night.

Starting under the warm afternoon sun at 3:00 PM, setting the tone with smooth melodic house,deep house ,soulful house ,Afro house, deep sunset grooves and your favourite sing alongs into the golden hour sequence.

EVENT DETAILS

• Time: 3:00 PM – 10:00 PM

• Venue: Bossa Hermanus (Outdoor Terrace)

• Music Style: Deep,tech ,soulful House, Melodic House & Afro House

• Dress Code: Summer Chic / Smart Casual

Ladies&Gents Make a effort

(No athletic sportswear, tracksuits, no gym caps, or casual beach slides)

• Table Bookings: DM @somos_evnts on Instagram

SAFETY & SECURITY

Dedicated security will be present throughout the event to ensure a safe, smooth, and enjoyable environment for everyone.

TERMS & CONDITIONS

• Media Consent: Filming and photography will take place throughout the event. By entering, you consent to being captured on photo and video for promotional and social media content.

• Disclaimer of Liability: Attendance is at your own risk. Somos Events and venue management accept no responsibility for loss, theft, or damage to personal property, or personal injury sustained on-site. Please celebrate responsibly.

We cannot wait to welcome you to the first edition of Somos

Bring the energy,make an effort with dress code and get ready for a seamless day-to-night journey.See you on December 20th!
