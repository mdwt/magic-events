## ABOUT

Begin the new year with an hour of Latin music performed by James Grace on the classical guitar.

Brian Berkman’s Pringle Proms presents this lunchtime recital at Pringle Bay United Church on

Saturday, 9 January 2027, from 12 noon to 1 pm.

A graduate of London’s Royal College of Music, Grace was the first guitarist to receive its Tagore Gold Medal. His album Andaluza – Music of Spain III won the 2019 South African Music Award for Best Instrumental Album. He returns to Pringle Proms following his Flavours of Spain recital in

April 2026.

Tickets: R200 in advance at Quicket; R250 cash or R280 by card at the door.
