## ABOUT

Merry Christmas! Get into the festive spirit at Peppermint Pottery and paint three of our cutest ceramic Christmas trees to hang on your own tree.

Pick your colours, add your own patterns and sparkle, and we'll take care of the glazing and firing. Pottery lasts forever, so these little trees will come out every December as a precious memory of the day you made them.

A lovely festive outing for families, friends and grandparents with grandkids.

Ages: 5–95, no experience needed · Includes: clay or paint, glazing & firing · Collection: 2–3 weeks after your session, or in the new year for sessions from 18 Dec. National courier available. · Bookings: 12 places per session. No show = no refund. Reschedule with 24 hours’ notice.
