## ABOUT

Lomond Summer Events

Our Lomond Summer Events are back for the 2026 festive season and run into our Blessing of the Harvest events in January 2027 during harvest time.

Hosted by our Cellarmaster Anné van Heerden.

Rooted in this land. Expressed in every glass.

Join us for a special wine-tasting experience beneath our Milkwood trees, celebrating the unique terroir, natural beauty and heritage of Cape Agulhas. Discover the character of our wines in a setting where the land tells its story, one glass at a time.

Cost:

Adults: R500 per person, includes the wine tasting and our substantial farm - to - table lunch.

Children: R300 per child under 18, includes lunch and a soft drink, no wine.

Time: Arrive at 12h00 at the Milkwood Forest, Lomond Farm.

The Milkwood Forest has ablutions and WiFi.

Fastest Fingers First as seats are limited.

Our Lomond Summer Events are always thoroughly enjoyed by our enthusiastic, wine-loving Cellar Club Members. We really hope you can join us this year.

Tel: 028 388 0095

Email: info@lomond.co.za

Whatsapp: 082 908 0098
