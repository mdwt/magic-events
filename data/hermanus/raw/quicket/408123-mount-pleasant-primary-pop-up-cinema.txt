## ABOUT

Hermanus FynArts presents a pop-up cinema fundraiser for Mount Pleasant Primary School, and it’s the very first outdoor movie night ever held at the school!

Join us as the sun sets over Hermanus for a screening of the much-loved animated film The Bad Guys 2 (PG).

The Bad Guys are struggling to find trust and acceptance in their newly minted lives as Good Guys, when they are pulled out of retirement and forced to do “one last job” by an all-female squad of criminals.

When: Monday 7 December 2026

Where: Mount Pleasant Primary School, Dahlia Street, Mount Pleasant, Hermanus

Gates open: 6pm

Movie starts: 7:30pm, at sunset

Tickets: R50 per person (general public)

Special ticket prices are available directly from the school for Mount Pleasant Primary students and parents ONLY.

Popcorn, hot dogs, sweets and soft drinks will be on sale on the night, so come hungry!

Arrive early to get a good spot, and bring your cushions, blankets and camping chairs to make yourselves comfy. Invite your friends and family, and let’s celebrate the holiday season together.

Every ticket helps raise funds for Mount Pleasant Primary School.

Weather

Outdoor cinema is weather-reliant. Should heavy rain or wind prevent us from hosting a safe and enjoyable outdoor movie night, we will move the cinema indoors to the school hall, so the show will go on, rain or shine!
