## ABOUT

Make your very own animal bowl! Using the age-old pinch-pot method, you'll shape a little bowl with your hands and then give it a character all of its own – add ears, a nose, legs or a long neck.

We glaze and fire it for you, so you'll take home a little friend to last forever. Great fun for little hands and big ones.

Ages: 5–95, no experience needed · Includes: clay or paint, glazing & firing · Collection: 2–3 weeks after your session, or in the new year for sessions from 18 Dec. National courier available. · Bookings: 12 places per session. No show = no refund. Reschedule with 24 hours’ notice.
