## ABOUT

An Afternoon of Music. An Evening to Savour.

Treat yourself to an unforgettable afternoon at the magnificent Benguela Cove Wine Estate, where breathtaking lagoon views, award-winning wines and world-class music combine to create a truly memorable experience.

Internationally acclaimed saxophonist Andrew Young has been entertaining audiences at Benguela Cove for the past 7 years, transforming beloved songs into unforgettable live performances. From Beatles classics and iconic movie themes to soul, rock and timeless favourites, every show is filled with energy, humour, nostalgia and exceptional musicianship.

One moment you'll be laughing at a story from Andrew's travels. The next, you'll be singing along to a song you haven't heard in years.

You don't need to love jazz or know anything about saxophones. You simply need to enjoy outstanding music in a spectacular setting.

Many people attend their first Andrew Young concert out of curiosity, they leave wondering when they can experience it again.

Extend the occasion with dinner from the popup Pizza Bar or from the menu of the elegant Moody Lagoon Restaurant, where superb cuisine, award-winning wines and panoramic lagoon views provide the perfect, remarkable day.

Come expecting a saxophone concert.

Leave having experienced so much more.

Book your tickets today.

Doors Open 16:30. Show 17:30
