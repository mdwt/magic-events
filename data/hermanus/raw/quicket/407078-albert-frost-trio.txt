## ABOUT

Wed 30 Dec @ 20h00 in our BOMA

Tickets: R250 on Quicket OR Tickets R280 @the Door (If still available)

STANDING Tickets Only - There are benches available but first come first serve

Catch SAMA award-winner Albert Frost live with his trio for a night of serious guitar work and classic South African blues-rock. Known for his intense stage presence and effortless shifts between heavy electric riffs and stripped-back acoustic grooves, Frost brings a tight, high-energy show that hits every mark. If you appreciate great musicianship, tight rhythm, and real live music, this is one gig you don't want to miss.

SHOW ONLY - FOOD Trucks Available for dinner @ Boma and Main Restaurant open

Seating operates on a first-come, first-served basis. Make sure to arrive early, as doors open at 5 pm.

Doors close at 7:45pm no late entry.

The Hangklip Hotel offers a range of room options for your stay. You can conveniently book online for a minimum 3 night stay at www.thehangkliphotel.com. If you're considering a longer stay, please reach out via email at bookings@thehangkliphotel.com for a special rate, especially if you're attending the show.

Whats app only for more information
