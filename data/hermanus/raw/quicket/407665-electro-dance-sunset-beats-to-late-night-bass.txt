## ABOUT

***Electro DANCE - Sunset Beats to Late Night Bass***

16 December @ 7pm

Tickets R100 on Quicket and R130 @ the Door (if still available)

NO UNDER 18 years

(Right of admission reserved)

As the summer sun dips below the horizon, join us for a journey through sound at the foot of Hangklip. We’re taking you from sun-drenched golden hour grooves all the way into deep-night energy - with Burgh, Snakes & Femme Phonix on the decks

We kick off in the warmth of the late afternoon with nice and bouncy downtempo as the sky turns to gold. As dusk settles, we elevate the vibe into organic house, soulful Afro house, and sparkling nu-disco - setting the stage for a late-night descent into high-energy tech house and driving rhythms.

Expect ocean breezes, coastal summer heat, heavy basslines, and non-stop dancing

The Hangklip Hotel offers a range of room options for your stay. You can conveniently book online for a 3night Stay at www.thehangkliphotel.com. If you're considering a longer stay, please reach out via email at

bookings@thehangkliphotel.com for a special rate, especially if you're attending the show.
