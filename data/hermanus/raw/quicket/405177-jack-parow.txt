## ABOUT

21 DEC @ 20:00 pm

Tickets R350 on Quicket and R380 at the Door (If still available)

The Ambassador of Good Times is back! JACK PAROW brings his legendary live show to the Hangklip Hotel.

Rapper, poet, and South African pop culture icon, Parow has redefined Afrikaans culture through relatable lyrics, razor-sharp social observation, and unstoppable energy. Grab your crew and prepare for a classic Hangklip jol!

This ticket grants you access to the performance only. Please arrive at least 30 minutes before the show to get through the queue and to find a standing spot in the venue. If you’d like to enjoy Dinner & SHOW, we recommend arriving well in advance from 5pm. NO FOOD during show **SHOW STARTS AT 8 PM.**

The Hangklip Hotel offers a range of room options for your stay. You can conveniently book online for minimum stay in December is 3 nights, if available at www.thehangkliphotel.com. If you're considering a longer stay, please reach out via email at bookings@thehangkliphotel.com for a special rate, especially if you're attending the show.
