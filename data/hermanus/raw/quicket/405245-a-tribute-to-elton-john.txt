## ABOUT

Mon 4 Jan, @ 20.00 pm

Ticket prices: R280 on Quicket and R320 at the door (IF any still available)

A Tribute To Elton John by Rocket Max (UK)

The ultimate celebration of a true musical megastar!

The man himself may have retired from touring, but it doesn’t mean we have to say Goodbye to that Yellow Brick Road just yet..!

Join Max Anthony (aka Rocket Max) and his sensational live band on a musical journey through the thrills and spills of Elton’s mesmerising career as they recreate the sound, feel and flamboyance of the Rocket Man in concert like it’s never been done before.

Performing all the hits - Your Song, Bennie & The Jets, Saturday Night’s Alright (For Fighting), I Guess That’s Why They Call It The Blues and MANY more… the piano will be pounded and the house will be rocked!

This show isn’t just another tribute act, it really is Elton… to the MAX.

Just the SHOW

This event requires pre-booked seating, and a floor plan is provided for you to select your specific seat. Please note that some seats offer a limited view as is indicated when you select seat number.

DINNER & SHOW

A **Mini Menu** will be offered for dinner selections beginning at **5 PM**. Please be aware that the last orders will be accepted at **7:00 PM**, and no food will be served during the show.

This ticket grants you access to the performance only. Please arrive at least 30 minutes before the show to get settled in. If you’d like to enjoy Dinner & SHOW, we recommend arriving well in advance. **SHOW STARTS AT 8 PM.**

In consideration of the artist, please refrain from speaking during the performance!

The Hangklip Hotel offers a range of room options for your stay. You can conveniently book online for a 2 nights at www.thehangkliphotel.com. If you're considering a longer stay, please reach out via email at bookings@thehangkliphotel.com for a special discounted rate, especially if you're attending the show.
