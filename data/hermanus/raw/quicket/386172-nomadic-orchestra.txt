## ABOUT

Fri 4 Dec, 20h00

Tickets: R160 Quicket / R200 at the door (If still available)

TICKET SALES CLOSE AT 2PM ON QUICKET ON 4 DEC 2026

Get ready to move! The unstoppable, high-energy brass monsters Nomadic Orchestra are taking over The Hangklip Hotel for a night of infectious rhythms, booming horns, and pure dancefloor magic. Blending Balkan beats, brass, hip-hop, and hard-hitting groove, they’re guaranteed to blow the roof off the coast. Grab your crew, pack your dancing shoes, and come get wild at the foot of the Hangklip!

This ticket grants you access to the performance only. Please arrive at least 15 minutes before the show to get settled in. If you’d like to enjoy Dinner & SHOW, we recommend arriving well in advance at 5 pm. **SHOW STARTS AT 8 PM.** no food service during the show, last orders 7 pm.

Seating operates on a first-come, first-served basis. Make sure to arrive early, as doors open at 5 pm.

The Hangklip Hotel offers a range of room options for your stay. You can conveniently book online for a single night at www.thehangkliphotel.com. If you're considering a longer stay, please reach out via email at bookings@thehangkliphotel.com for a special rate, especially if you're attending the show.
