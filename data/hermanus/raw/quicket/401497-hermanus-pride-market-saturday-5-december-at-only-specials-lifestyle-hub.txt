## ABOUT

Pride is coming to Hermanus! Join to celebrate by supporting LGBT small businesses at our Hermanus Pride Market on Saturday Dec 5 from 10 am to 4 pm at Only Special Lifestyles Hub.

FREE entry

Enjoy LGBT vendors offering unique Christmas gifts, food, drinks and cocktails!

After the market we will enjoy the Hermanus pride march and party!
