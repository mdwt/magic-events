## ABOUT

SAVE THE DATE for this NYE 2026

Thursday 31 Dec @ 9pm

Band (3 sets of 45min)

Doors open 8:00pm

Ticket Price: R300 (December) On Quicket and R350 @ The Door (if still available)

EARLY BIRD PRICE: R200 (November)

Teenagers Tickets 12 years - 18 years Early Bird Price: R100 (November)

Get ready to step back into the groovy era of flare pants, platform shoes, and glitter!

Join us at the legendary Hangklip Hotel as we ring in the New Year with an unforgettable 70s-themed party. We’ve got the incredible DownTown Gypsies hitting the stage to keep the dance floor alive all night with incredible live music and high-energy vibes.

Dust off your best vintage threads, dig out the sequined jumpsuits, and pull on those bell-bottoms!

Come celebrate at one of the coast's most iconic landmark hotels for a night of retro beats, cold drinks, and good times with great people. Let’s bring in the New Year in true 70s style!

SHOW ONLY - (FOOD TRUCKS Available and Restaurant bookings open)

Seating operates on a first-come, first-served basis. Make sure to arrive early, as doors open at 8 pm.

The Hangklip Hotel offers a range of room options for your stay. You can conveniently book online for 3 night stay at www.thehangkliphotel.com. If you're considering a longer stay, please reach out via email at bookings@thehangkliphotel.com for a special rate, especially if you're attending the show.

Whats app only for more information
