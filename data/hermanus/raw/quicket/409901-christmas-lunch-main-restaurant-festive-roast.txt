## ABOUT

The Festive Xmas Roast

Event name

The Festive Xmas Roast – Christmas Done Right

Short description

A relaxed, merry Christmas Day lunch at The Hangklip Hotel, Pringle Bay: crispy roast pork belly with all the trimmings, bright summer flavours and plenty of festive cheer.

Date and time

Friday, 25 December 2026

Lunch served from 13:00

Venue

Main Hotel, The Hangklip Hotel, Pringle Bay, Western Cape

# DESCRIPTION

Ho ho hold the oven gloves – this Christmas, we're doing the cooking! Grab the family, round up your favourite people and join us at the Main Hotel for a summer roast table that's all about golden crackling, bright, zesty flavours, slow roasts and good company. No stress, no dishes, just a long, lazy festive lunch by the sea. Christmas, done right.

## To Kick Things Off – Starter

- Grilled Peach & Whipped Feta Crostini: juicy, chargrilled peaches on crisp crostini with silky whipped feta, fresh basil and a drizzle of golden honey.

## The Star of the Show – Main

- Crispy Apple Orchard Roast Pork Belly: tender, slow-roasted pork belly with proper golden crackling, served with:

- Rosemary roast potatoes, crispy on the outside and fluffy inside

- Crisp apple and fennel salad

- Buttered garden greens

- Our homemade apple relish

- A rich mustard pan jus

## A Little Something Sweet – Dessert

- Lemon Posset with Passion Fruit: a silky, zingy lemon posset topped with tangy passion fruit and served with buttery homemade shortbread.

# Ticket types

TicketPrice

Starter + MainR302.50 per person

Ticket prices include a 10% staff gratuity on the food only. Drinks are bought separately from your waitron on the day, and the gratuity on drinks is at your discretion.
