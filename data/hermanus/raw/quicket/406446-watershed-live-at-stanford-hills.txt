## ABOUT

Just a stone’s throw from Hermanus, Stanford Hills is one of South Africa’s favourite live music venues, set among green lawns with a beautiful view of the mountains.

Join Watershed for an afternoon of big tunes, a big stage and the kind of sunset that makes you want to stay a little longer. We’d love to see you there.

THINGS TO NOTE:

GATES OPEN: 3:00 pm

SUPPORT ACT: 4:30PM

WATERSHED on 6PM

Venue: Stanford Hills, Stanford

Tickets

Adults: R280–R360

Children (5–13): R180–R220

Children under 5: Free

NO FOOD OR DRINK TO BE BROUGHT INTO THE VENUE - THIS IS AVAILABLE THROUGH THE VENUE

Guests may bring picnic blankets and low chairs for the grass. There are shade areas available on first come first served basis
