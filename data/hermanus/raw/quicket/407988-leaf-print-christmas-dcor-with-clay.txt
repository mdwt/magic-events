## ABOUT

The perfect holiday activity! Get creative in our studio as we head outside to pick our favourite leaves, then learn how to press them into clay to leave beautiful natural prints.

We'll cut our clay into festive shapes, and once we've glazed your pieces in soft blues, whites and greens and fired them, you'll have your own handmade décor to keep forever.

Please note: there is no painting in this session.

Ages: 5–95, no experience needed · Includes: clay or paint, glazing & firing · Collection: 2–3 weeks after your session, or in the new year for sessions from 18 Dec. National courier available. · Bookings: 12 places per session. No show = no refund. Reschedule with 24 hours’ notice.
