## ABOUT

Join us in the picturesque Hemel & Aarde Valley at Bartho Eksteen wine Estate just outside Hermanus, Western Cape on 20 December 2026 11am to 5pm to enjoy the wines of the Hidden Gem winemakers of the valley and surrounds, live music and foodie stalls' fare. Bring friends & family along and enjoy a relaxed day on the farm, there's lots of space for a picnic blanket and kids can roam safely.
