## ABOUT

Experience an uplifting, resonant evening of live string quartet music in the heart of Onrusrivier!

Join Southern Light Strings at NG Kerk Onrusrivier on Saturday , 12 December 2026 for a special stop on The Festive Pop-Up Series.

Set inside the warm, natural acoustics of the church, our curated 75-minute sunset programme celebrates the joy of a South African summer Christmas. Enjoy timeless classical masterpieces (Bach’s Jesu, Joy of Man’s Desiring and Corelli’s Christmas Concerto), beloved carols (Carol of the Bells, Silent Night), upbeat Bridgerton-style holiday pop crossovers (All I Want For Christmas, Feliz Navidad), and iconic South African summer favourites like Somerkersfees and Mango Groove’s Special Star.

Whether you are a local resident or visiting Hermanus/Onrus for the December holidays, bring the whole family for an unforgettable musical celebration!

Your Quartet for the Evening

• Violin I: Elma Salt

• Violin II: Petra Magner

• Viola: Maja van Dyk

• Cello: Maya Maile

Evening Schedule (Saturday, 12 December 2026)

• 17:00 — Church Doors Open (Unreserved seating - arrive early to choose your preferred seats)

• 18:00 – 19:15 — Live String Quartet Performance (75-minute continuous set, no intermission)

• 19:15 onwards — Evening concludes

Ticket Options & Concessions

• General Admission (Adult) - R250: Standard unreserved seating.

• Pensioner / Student - R180: Discounted unreserved seating (valid Pensioner 60+ or student card may be requested at the door).

• Child (Under 12) - R120: Discounted unreserved seating for children under 12 (children under 4 enter free when seated on a parent’s lap).

Venue & Practical Info

• Location: NG Kerk Onrusrivier (Main Halll/Hoofsaal), 55 Berg Street, Onrusrivier, Hermanus 7201

• Seating: All seating is unreserved (first-come, first-served) inside the main church building.

• Church Guidelines: To honour the church building and congregation guidelines, no food, wine, or alcohol is permitted inside.

Seats are limited—book early to secure your spot!
