## ABOUT

Event name

Asado de Navidad – Christmas Lunch Feast on Fire

Short description

An Argentine-inspired Christmas Day lunch cooked over open fire at The Hangklip Hotel, Pringle Bay: slow-fired meats, fire-roasted sides and festive communal tables beneath Hangklip Mountain.

Date and time

Friday, 25 December 2026

Arrival: 12:30 | Feast served from 13:30

Venue

The Boma, The Hangklip Hotel, Pringle Bay, Western Cape

# DESCRIPTION

The Hangklip Hotel invites you to celebrate Christmas Day with Asado de Navidad, our Argentine-inspired Christmas feast. In Argentina, the asado is more than a meal. It is a celebration of fire, family and friends gathered around the coals for a long, slow afternoon. We bring that tradition to Pringle Bay, with meats hung and slow-roasted over open flames and glowing embers, served at our festive communal tables in the Boma, beneath Hangklip Mountain.

## From the First Embers – Starters

- Log-Seared Ostrich Fillet: whole ostrich fillet seared over the coals and thinly carved, with watercress, baby spinach, wild rocket and a berry-balsamic glaze.

- Garlic White Wine Mussel Bowl: whole-shell mussels simmered in garlic, white wine and butter with fresh herbs, served with fire-toasted buttered flatbread.

## The Main Fire – The Asado Feast

- Slow-Fired Tomahawk: bone-in beef tomahawks slowly roasted over wood embers, then flame-seared and carved into slivers.

- The Hanging Chooks: whole free-range chickens hung over the fire and basted with butter until golden.

- Pistachio & Herb Porchetta: pork belly rolled with pistachio and herb pesto, slow-roasted over the coals and carved into slices.

## From the Coals & Earth – Accompaniments

- Coal-Roasted Beetroot with bitter leaves and a light dressing

- Whole Fire-Roasted Pumpkin with local honey and balsamic reduction

- Hung Burnt Cabbage & Cauli Slaw with fresh herbs and burnt-citrus dressing

- Fire-Crispy Smashed Potatoes with aromatic ghee and confit garlic

## The Last Embers – Dessert

- Warm Fire-Baked Summer Fruit: seasonal fruit baked over the coals, with golden cinnamon crumble and cold homemade custard.

# IMPORTANT INFORMATION

- All bookings are made online through Quicket. For more information, call 028 273 8310 or WhatsApp 084 546 5214.

- Children under 7 are seated on a parent's lap and do not have their own place setting. Parents are asked to provide their child's food. Please let us know when booking if you will be bringing a child under 7.

- The menu is set and served as a communal feast. Please let us know if you are vegetarian as soon as you have booked so we can make a note. Last chance to confirm a vegetarian version is 7 days before event.

- Drinks are not included in the ticket price and can be bought from your waitron at the table on the day.

- Tickets are non-refundable but may be transferred to another guest. Please let us know the new guest's name before the event.

- The Hangklip Hotel may make minor changes to the menu depending on what is in season.
