## ABOUT

Wind down the holidays with a relaxed session of nature and clay. We’ll collect leaves from outside, play and learn about the patterns they leave in clay, and cut our pieces into fun shapes.

We glaze them in soft blues, whites and greens and fire them for you, so you’ll have a handmade decoration to keep forever.

Please note: there is no painting in this session. Ages: 5–95, no experience needed · Includes: clay or paint, glazing & firing · Collection: 2–3 weeks after your session, or in the new year for sessions from 18 Dec. National courier available. · Bookings: 12 places per session. No show = no refund. Reschedule with 24 hours’ notice.
