## ABOUT

Join Brian Berkman at home in Pringle Bay for a relaxed morning of low-carb

cooking, followed by lunch. Watch him demonstrate a generous summer menu,

with practical techniques and ideas to bring to your own table when entertaining

family and friends.

ON THE MENU

• Courgette soup, delicious served hot or cold

• Fail-proof whole roasted fillet of beef with three sauces

• Spiked cauliflower rice salad

• Asian salad made with cauliflower noodles

• Cheese platter

Bring your own wines and other special drinks.

Participants will be emailed a recipe booklet.

Watch, learn and enjoy lunch together.
