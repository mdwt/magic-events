## ABOUT

Fri. 18 Dec @ 20.00pm

Tickets at the Door = R300 (If available)

Tickets on Quicket = R270

TICKET SALES CLOSE AT 2PM ON QUICKET ON 18 DEC 2026

Bounce! Get ready to boogie and jump to the high-energy, genre-bending circus rock of Crimson House at The Hangklip Hotel. Blending gritty blues-rock, gypsy punk, and infectious ska rhythm, their signature sound hits like a freight train - delivering soaring brass, foot-stomping grooves, and raw, theatrical stage presence. It’s a full-throttle sonic party guaranteed to keep the floor moving until the final encore!

This ticket grants you access to the performance only. Please arrive at least 15 minutes before the show to get settled in. If you’d like to enjoy Dinner & SHOW, we recommend arriving well in advance from 5pm.

**SHOW STARTS AT 8 PM.**
