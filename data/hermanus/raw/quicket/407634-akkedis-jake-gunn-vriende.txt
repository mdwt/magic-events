## ABOUT

Sat 26 December 14.00 @ The Boma

Tickets: R200 on Quicket / R230 at the door (IF STILL AVAILABLE)

Na al die Kersfees-kos en familieverpligtinge is dit tyd om uit die huis te kom! Akkedis, Jake Gunn en vriende sorg vir 'n goeie dosis Suid-Afrikaanse blues-rock en oorspronklike musiek. Bring jou vriende, kry 'n koue een, en kom geniet 'n lekker feesdag onder die Klip by die see!

After all the Christmas food and family obligations, it’s time to get out of the house! Akkedis, Jake Gunn, and friends will provide a good dose of South African blues-rock and original music. Bring your friends, grab a cold one, and come enjoy a great festival day under the Rock by the sea!

SHOW ONLY - FOOD Trucks outside Boma and Lunch available at Restaurant

Seating operates on a first-come, first-served basis. Make sure to arrive early, as doors open at 12 pm.

Doors close at 2pm no late entry.

The Hangklip Hotel offers a range of room options for your stay. You can conveniently book online for a minimum 3 night stay at www.thehangkliphotel.com. If you're considering a longer stay, please reach out via email at bookings@thehangkliphotel.com for a special rate, especially if you're attending the show.

Whats app only for more information

Deborah 084 546 5214

Tanya 060 663 1288

Marius 063 348 3521
