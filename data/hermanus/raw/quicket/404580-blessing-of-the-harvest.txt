## ABOUT

Blessing of the Harvest 2027 - In collaboration with Lost Boy Wines

Lomond Summer Events.

Join us as we raise a glass to the season ahead and bless the 2027 harvest.

This special tasting celebrates the promise of a new vintage, a moment of gratitude for the soil, the vines, and the hands that nurture them. Sip through a curated selection of Lomond and Lost Boy wines that reflect the journey from vine to bottle, each one a reminder of cellar stories and the beauty of patience.

Together, let’s toast to a fruitful harvest, great wines, and the blessings of another year among the vines.

Arrive at our Milkwoods Forest venue at 12:00am.

Here you will enjoy a wine tasting hosted by Lomond Cellarmaster Anné van Heerden and Lost Boy Wines' Trevor DeRuisé, with our Farm-to-Table lunch set out under the Milkwoods canopy in true Cape Agulhas style.

Cost:

Adults: R500 per person, includes the wine tasting and farm - to - table lunch.

Children: R300 per child under 18, includes lunch and a soft drink, no wine.

Time: Arrive at 12h00 at our Milkwoods Forest venue. The event will end by 15h00.

The Milkwood Forest has ablutions and WiFi.

Fastest Fingers First as seats are limited.

Our Lomond Summer Events are always thoroughly enjoyed by our enthusiastic, wine-loving Cellar Club Members. We really hope you can join us this year.

Tel: 028 388 0095

Email: info@lomond.co.za

Whatsapp: 082 908 0098
