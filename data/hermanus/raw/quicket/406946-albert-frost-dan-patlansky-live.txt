## Stanford Hills Sunset Sessions

### Albert Frost & Dan Patlansky, Live

Sunday, 20 December 2026 | Stanford Hills, Stanford

Two of South Africa’s most celebrated guitarists share one stage for a single afternoon and evening in the Overberg.

Albert Frost and Dan Patlansky are known for fearless, soulful playing, blending blues, rock and roots into sets that feel raw and alive. Each has built a loyal following over years of touring and recording. Together, they promise something rarer: a combined show where two distinct voices trade licks, swap spotlights and push each other into the kind of spontaneous moments you only get live.

Doors open at 3 PM, so arrive early, claim your spot and soak up the relaxed atmosphere of Stanford Hills. Food vendors and a cash bar will be on site, so you can graze and sip while the afternoon unfolds. Bring a picnic blanket or fold-out chair and settle in. Then, as the sun sets over the Overberg, expect searing solos, heartfelt songs and plenty of crowd favourites. Bring your friends, your family and your festive spirit, and kick off the December holidays with a night of world-class guitar.

Don’t miss it. Book early.

Stanford Hills, Stanford, Western Cape

Sunday, 20 December 2026

Doors open 3:00 PM | Show 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM

Adults R300 | Kids (5 to 16 years) R200

Food vendors and cash bar on site

Bring a picnic blanket or fold-out chair
