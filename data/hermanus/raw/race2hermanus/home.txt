Race Rules & Terms

## Know Before You Race

Race2Hermanus is sanctioned by Triathlon South Africa (TSA) and the Cape Town Triathlon Association (CTTA), and governed by World Triathlon Rules and Regulations. R2H is a non-draft-legal event - TT bikes and tribars are permitted. All participants are bound by the EIM Terms, Conditions and Indemnity. The pre-race briefing on race morning takes precedence over all published documentation.

01TSA Licensing

+

- All participants must hold a valid TSA licence to compete

- Annual TSA licence holders are already covered - no day licence fee charged

- No annual licence? A day licence fee is added to your entry at registration

- Day licences cover public liability for the specific event only

- EIM strongly recommends personal accident and medical insurance in addition to TSA cover

02Cycling Rules

+

R2H is NON-DRAFT-LEGAL. TT bikes and tribars are permitted.

- Strict non-drafting rules apply - maintain required draft zone distance at all times

- TT bikes and aerobars/tribars are permitted - all bar ends must be plugged

- Drafting violations penalised by TSA Technical Officials

- Approved hard-shell helmet mandatory - no helmet, no ride

- Helmet must remain clipped on for the entire cycle leg

- No cycling permitted in or around the transition area

03Swim Rules

+

- Swim buoys (pull buoys) are not permitted

- Race numbers may not be worn during the swim leg

- A brightly coloured swim cap is mandatory for all participants

- EIM does not always provide a cap - bring your own if not confirmed in your race pack

- Athletes must complete the swim course as marked - cutting the course results in disqualification

04Age Categories

+

Age is determined by the athlete's age as at 31 December of the race year - not age on race day.

- Sprint: must turn 16 by 31 December 2027

- Standard & Ultra: must turn 18 by 31 December 2027

- Minimum age requirements are absolute - parental consent does not override TSA rules

05Equipment & Devices

+

- Headphones and personal audio devices are strictly forbidden

- Action cameras require prior written permission from EIM

- Mobile phones for emergency use only - stored away during the race

- Each athlete is responsible for the safety of their own equipment

Unauthorised camera or phone use during the race may result in disqualification.

06Timing Chip

+

- A timing chip is issued to each participant

- Chips must be returned at the end of the event

- Lost or unreturned chips charged at a replacement cost of R400

07Refund & Transfer Policy

+

WhenRefund

More than 6 weeks before eventFull entry less 6% admin + R200 handling

4 weeks before event50% of entry fee

Less than 4 weeks beforeNo refund

- Substitutions allowed at no charge up to 4 weeks before the event

- Category changes before deadline: R150 admin fee applies

- No informal transfers - official process only via events@electricinkmedia.co.za

08Conduct & Safety

+

- No unsportsmanlike behaviour - abuse of officials or competitors may result in disqualification

- No unauthorised person may run or cycle on the active race course

- Transition is restricted - registered participants and accredited officials only

- TSA Technical Officials' and race organisers' decisions are final

- Pre-race briefing on race morning takes precedence over all published rules

Participating under another athlete's name results in disqualification from this and all future EIM events.
