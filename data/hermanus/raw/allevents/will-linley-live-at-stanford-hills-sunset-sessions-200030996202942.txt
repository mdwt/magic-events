## About the event

Will Linley Live at Stanford Hills Sunset Sessions

Get ready for an unforgettable summer afternoon as Will Linley takes the stage at the beautiful Stanford Hills Estate this December!

Join us for a magical, family-friendly outdoor concert in the heart of the Overberg, where incredible live music, breathtaking scenery and those feel-good summer vibes come together!

One of South Africa’s most exciting musical talents, Will Linley is bringing his infectious energy, heartfelt lyrics and sing-along anthems to Stanford for a very special live performance.

Bring your friends, gather the family and get ready to dance, sing and soak up the sunshine as we celebrate everything we love about summer in South Africa!

You may also like the following events from Stanford Hills Estate:

- This Saturday, 28th November, 12:00 pm, Stanford Stumble in Gansbaai

- Next month, 15th December, 03:00 pm, Zolani Mahola + The Feminine Force (featuring Karen Zoid) live at Stanford Hills Sunset Sessions in Strand

- Next month, 18th December, 03:00 pm, Dreaming out Loud ft Neon Dream & Byrne in Gansbaai
