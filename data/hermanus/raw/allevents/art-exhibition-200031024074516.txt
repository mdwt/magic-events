Art exhibition

✨ SAVE THE DATE! ✨

27 November – 1 December 2026

The Hangklip Art Collective is coming together!

A celebration of creativity, talent and artistic expression, bringing together a collection of artists from across the Hangklip area.

Join us at the beautiful Harold Porter National Botanical Garden as we share our work, our stories and our love of art.

Come and discover the incredible creative talent right here in our community.

More details coming soon!

We look forward to seeing you there. 🎨🌿
