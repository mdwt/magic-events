Ballyhoo: The 50 Year Celebration LIVE IN HERMANUS!

South African music legends Ballyhoo celebrate an extraordinary five decades in the music industry with their spectacular 50 Years Celebration Tour.

This unforgettable live show is a celebration of the music, memories, and timeless spirit of rock ’n’ roll that has connected generations of fans. Join Ballyhoo this December and be part of a remarkable musical milestone—50 years of songs, stories, and unforgettable performances.
