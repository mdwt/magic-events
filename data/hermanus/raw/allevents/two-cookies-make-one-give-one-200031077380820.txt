## About the event

TWO COOKIES™️ | MAKE ONE. GIVE ONE.

TWO COOKIES™ — MAKE ONE. GIVE ONE.

Small hands. Big kindness. A whole lot of heart!

Tonight, we're opening the doors to something very special.

Imagine little hands covered in flour, laughter around a baking table, and a child proudly making two Christmas cookies.

One for themselves.

And one for somebody they may never meet.

Somebody who might need a little kindness this Christmas.

That's where our story begins.

On Wednesday, 16 December 2026, we're inviting everyone to Wonderland Lifestyle Nursery & Play Park in Hermanus for a wonderful day of baking, laughter and giving.

Our dream? 2,000 cookies made. 1,000 cookies shared.

For just R50, you'll receive everything needed to make your two cookies. Baking sessions run hourly from 9 AM to 2 PM, with an optional R50 play-park voucher.

You can book and pay online and download your own Certificate of Kindness.

Can't attend? You can still make a difference! Sponsor a baking pack, have cookies baked on your behalf, or contribute to our Christmas Cookie Bank.

And if your family could use a little Christmas kindness, please complete our Cookie Bank claim form. You can also nominate somebody else who might appreciate a little Christmas cheer.

A heartfelt thank you to Wonderland for welcoming us, Eggcellent Eggs for donating eggs, and Golden Reef Mills / BIO-WHEAT™ for beautiful stoneground flour!

We're still looking for butter, sugar and helping hands. Even the smallest contribution matters.

And come meet Cookie 1 and Cookie 2, our two delightful little characters! They're waiting on our website with their own musical adventure, and they need YOUR help choosing their names. There's a prize of two baking packs for the winning entry!

We spend so much time thinking about what we need or what we haven't got.

But what if, just for a moment, we asked ourselves something different?

What can I do for somebody else?

Sometimes, it can be as simple as making somebody a cookie.

And perhaps that's the greatest gift we can teach our children this Christmas.

Not just how to bake. But how to give.

Welcome to our story. We'd love you to be part of it.
