## About the event

JINGLE BELL ROCK with The Family Band at Hermanus Golf Club

This festive season, get ready for an unforgettable night of music, nostalgia, laughter, and holiday magic with The Family Band presents Jingle Bell Rock

Jingle Bell Rock is a spectacular Christmas variety show guaranteed to get audiences singing, dancing, and celebrating the spirit of the season.

Featuring a powerhouse 8-piece live band, Jingle Bell Rock brings together the greatest Christmas hits and timeless classics from some of the world’s most beloved artists. Audiences can look forward to festive favourites from legendary names like Wham, Boney M, Bing Crosby, Frank Sinatra, and Johnny Mathis, alongside energetic rock anthems, nostalgic 90’s medleys, Disney favourites, and even music inspired by the iconic Home Alone soundtrack.

More than just a concert, Jingle Bell Rock is a fully scripted musical journey that captures the memories, emotions, and traditions that make Christmas so special. The production combines dazzling performances, rich vocal harmonies, soaring guitar solos, and storytelling to create a magical theatre experience for the whole family.

The show stars acclaimed performers including Dewald Louw, Dee Dee Theunissen and The Family Band Trio, delivering unforgettable renditions of the songs audiences know and love.

Whether you want to get up and dance, sing along to festive classics, or simply soak in the holiday atmosphere, Jingle Bell Rock promises world-class live entertainment filled with Christmas cheer and Rock n Roll!
