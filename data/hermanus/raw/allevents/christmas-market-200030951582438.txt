## About the event

Christmas Market

Where are the weeks going!! We cannot believe it is nearly time for our wonderful outdoor Christmas market.

We have lots of new stalls and will still have the popular ones bringing new fresh stock.

There will be live music and the tent over the lawn to meet family and friends and enjoy a meal from our food vendors.

Please diarise and make it another day to remember.
