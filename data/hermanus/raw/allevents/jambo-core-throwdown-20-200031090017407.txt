## About the event

Jambo core Throwdown 2.0

JAMBO CORE THROWDOWN 2.0

A RACE AFTER CHRISTMAS.

📅 26 December 2026

📍 Hermanus Cricket Ground

⏰ 8AM–12PM

🎫 R450 per athlete.

A hybrid race testing strength, endurance, stamina & teamwork.

RACE FORMAT

7 workout stations - each separated by a 500m run.

CATEGORIES

• Men’s Doubles

• Women’s Doubles

• Mixed Doubles

RULES

• Movement standards must be met

• No rep = no count

• Complete all prescribed work

• Doubles stay together during the runs.

If you have any questions to understand anything about the race don’t hesitate to reach out.
