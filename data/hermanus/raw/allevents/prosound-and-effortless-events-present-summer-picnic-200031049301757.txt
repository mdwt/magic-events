## About the event

Prosound and Effortless Events present Summer Picnic

This December, we're bringing the community together for an unforgettable SUMMER PICNIC at Die Bron Primary School!

Entertainment: Featuring our very own LOCAL DJs bringing you the best music, good vibes, and non-stop entertainment! Expect great music, summer vibes, good company, and an amazing atmosphere!

Let's come together, support our local talent, and make memories this festive season! Spread the word, tag your friends, and get ready!

Tickets Will Be Sold From 23 November to 4 December @Die Bron Primary School(Cash Only).

Saturday, 19 December. Gates open at 12:00
