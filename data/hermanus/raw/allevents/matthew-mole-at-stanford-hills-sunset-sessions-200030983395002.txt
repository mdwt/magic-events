## About the event

Matthew Mole at Stanford Hills Sunset Sessions

Our Sunset Sessions are all about connection — families relaxing on the lawn, kids dancing barefoot, and friends sharing laughter as the sun dips behind the hills. Over the years, we’ve welcomed some of South Africa’s most loved artists to our stage, always in the spirit of warmth, community, and celebration.

Come experience the magic for yourself.

Important information:

Bring a picnic blanket. All ages are welcome, and children under five can enter free of charge. Please note that outside food and drinks are not permitted.

(We’ve got you covered with delicious food vendors and a full cash bar)

- Doors Open at 15:00

- 16:00-16:45 Anica

- 17:00-18:00 Tree 63

- 18:30-20:00 Matthew Mole

- 20:00 Event Ends

Venue

Stanford Hills Estate, Weltevrede Farm, Stanford
