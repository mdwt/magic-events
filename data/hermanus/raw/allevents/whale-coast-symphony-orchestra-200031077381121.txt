## About the event

Whale Coast Symphony Orchestra

Join us for an enchanting open-air evening as the Whale Coast Symphony Orchestra from neighbouring Hermanus takes us on an amble through the greatest compositions of the classical repertoire. Settle into your spot with blankets, cushions or camping chairs and enjoy timeless melodies under the Walker Bay sky at Landskein.

Time: Gates open at 16:30 | Concert starts at 18:30.

Landskein wines, refreshing drinks, wood-fired pizza, popcorn and snacks will be available for purchase.

Please note: No outside food or beverages are permitted.

Should adverse weather conditions prevent the event from going ahead, guests will be notified via email.

Tickets: https://www.webtickets.co.za/v2/event.aspx?itemid=1607764088Tickets on Webtickets.

Enquiries: Allen 076 390 7443 | 028 054 0422
