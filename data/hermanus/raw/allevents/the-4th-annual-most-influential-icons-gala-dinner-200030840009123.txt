## About the event

The 4th Annual Most Influential Icons Gala Dinner

Valid Dreamers Foundation NPC in partnership with Creation Wines & the Overstrand Municipality Socio-Economic Services Unit to host the 4th Annual Most Influential Icons Gala Dinner 2026 ✨

An unforgettable evening of elegance, inspiration, and celebration awaits.

Join us for the 4th Annual Most Influential Icons Gala Dinner, where we honour excellence, connect with young and influential icons of the Overberg, and celebrate the remarkable individuals shaping our communities and contributing to socio-economic development.

📅 Friday, 4 December 2026

🕔 5:00 PM

📍 Overstrand Municipal Auditorium/Banqueting Hall, Hermanus

🎟️ Tickets

• General: R370

• VIP: R500

• General for 2: R700

Enjoy an evening of networking, entertainment, fine food, and Wine.

Seats are limited—secure your ticket and be part of a night to remember.
