## About the event

Arno Carstens Live at Sandbaai Saal, Hermanus

Hermanus, the African Sunrise Summer Tour is coming to Sandbaai Saal on Wednesday 30 December 🌅

Arno Carstens live with songs from his solo albums and the Springbok Nude Girls years. A summer evening of music in the heart of the Overberg.

The show starts at 6pm. There's a cash bar and a food truck on the night.

Tickets are limited, so book yours now 🎟️

https://www.quicket.co.za/events/407250-arno-carstens-african-sunrise-hermanus
