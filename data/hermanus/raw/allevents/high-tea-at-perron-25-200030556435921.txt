## About the event

High Tea at Perron 25

KLEINMOND'S MOST ICONIC HIGH TEA

Make sure your trip to the Overstrand is complete with the best High Tea Kleinmond has to offer.

Our historic High Tea, which takes place within our tea garden, promises an indulgent feast. While you dine on sumptuous sweets, pastries and sandwiches stacked in tiers, live piano music sets the scene.

starting from R120 per adult,

Wednesday, 10am-3pm with seating slots by the hour
