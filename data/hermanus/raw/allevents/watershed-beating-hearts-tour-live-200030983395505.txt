## About the event

Watershed Beating Hearts Tour Live

The Beating Hearts Tour - Live in This Town. Watershed is one of the country’s best loved live bands and Stanford Hills, one of South Africa’s favourite live music venues… what better way to spend the day. Just a stone’s throw from Hermanus, rolling green lawns with a beautiful view of the mountains.

Join Watershed and special guest Keenan Eksteen for an afternoon of big tunes, a big stage and the kind of sunset that makes you want to stay a little longer. We’d love to see you there.

- Next month, 18th December, 03:00 pm, Dreaming out Loud ft Neon Dream & Byrne in Gansbaai
