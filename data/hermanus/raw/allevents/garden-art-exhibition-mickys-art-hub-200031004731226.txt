## About the event

Garden Art Exhibition Mickys Art Hub

🎨 MICKY’S ART HUB ART STUDIO

STUDENTS’ ART OF THE YEAR – GARDEN EXHIBITION

🌸 21 Artists • One Beautiful Garden Exhibition • A Celebration of Creation and Creativity

Join us for a special afternoon celebrating the wonderful artistic talents of the students of Micky’s Art Hub Art Studio!

Our garden will become an outdoor art gallery, with 21 talented artists displaying their beautiful creations. Come and admire their hard work, imagination, passion and artistic journeys.

This is more than just an art exhibition. It is a celebration of creativity, encouragement and the belief that everyone deserves an opportunity to explore their artistic talents.

❤️ ENTRY IS FREE!

We warmly welcome donations from anyone who would like to support children who have artistic talent but whose families cannot afford art supplies.

Your contribution will help provide paints, brushes, canvases and other essential materials, giving these young artists the opportunity to learn, create and develop their gifts.

**Every donation, big or small, makes a difference!**☕ FREE REFRESHMENTS

Enjoy complimentary tea, coffee and snacks while you wander through the garden gallery, meet the artists and celebrate their achievements.

📍 EVENT DETAILS

Date: Saturday, 28 November 2026

Time: 11:00 – 15:00

Venue: Micky’s Art Hub Art Studio

Address: 40 Broadway, Fisherhaven

Entry: FREE

Tea, coffee & snacks: FREE

Donations: Welcome and greatly appreciated

RSVP: ESSENTIAL – Please reserve your place!

🌦️ PLEASE NOTE – WEATHER DEPENDENT

As this is an outdoor exhibition, all artworks will be suspended on strings around the garden. In the event of excessively strong winds or heavy rain, the exhibition will be postponed to protect the artwork.

Please look out for event updates before travelling.

Come and support our 21 artists, enjoy a lovely afternoon and help us give talented children the opportunity to discover the joy of art!

With love,

Micky

Micky’s Art Hub Art Studio 🎨
