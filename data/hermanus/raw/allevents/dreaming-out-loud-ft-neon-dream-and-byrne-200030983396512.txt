Dreaming out Loud ft Neon Dream & Byrne

DREAMING OUT LOUD FESTIVAL 2026 Ft NEON DREAMS & BYRNE

Get ready for an unforgettable summer as Dreaming Out Loud Festival returns to South Africa this December, bringing incredible live music, feel-good energy and unforgettable moments to the coast! Headlining this year's festival are Canadian favourites Neon Dreams, alongside the incredibly talented South African singer-songwriter Byrne.
