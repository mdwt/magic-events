Sandbaai Market

The Sandbaai Market brings the community together for an afternoon of local shopping, good food and family fun. With more than 40 unique vendors, there is plenty to discover, whether you are looking for something tasty to eat, a special gift or a relaxed way to spend time with friends and family.

Browse a wide range of stalls featuring delicious food, desserts, fresh fruit and vegetables, cool drinks and a full bar. You’ll also find crafts made by local creators, handmade soaps and lotions, knitted items, artwork, jewellery, children’s toys and thrift finds. Each market offers a chance to support small businesses, meet local makers and discover something new.

The Sandbaai Market takes place on the first Saturday of each month from 14:00 to 19:00 at Sandbaai Hall. The hall offers plenty of indoor space, so visitors can enjoy the market in comfort even when the weather is rainy or windy. When conditions allow, children can also enjoy a free bouncy castle.

Make an afternoon of it: bring the family, invite friends and take your time exploring the stalls. Whether you come for a meal, a browse or a catch-up with neighbours, the market is a welcoming place to enjoy local community spirit while supporting local vendors.

The Sandbaai Market is organised by the Sandbaai Community Voluntary Improvement District NPC for the benefit of the local community and local vendors.
