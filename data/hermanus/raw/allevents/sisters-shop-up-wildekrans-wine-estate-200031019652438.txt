## About the event

sisters. SHOP-UP @ Wildekrans Wine Estate

🌸✨ 𝙨𝙞𝙨𝙩𝙚𝙧𝙨. 𝙎𝙃𝙊𝙋-𝙐𝙋 𝙞𝙨 𝙩𝙖𝙠𝙞𝙣𝙜 𝙤𝙣 𝙩𝙝𝙚 𝙒𝙚𝙨𝙩𝙚𝙧𝙣 𝘾𝙖𝙥𝙚! ✨🌸

After a successful first time at Wildekrans Wine Estate last year December, sisters. SHOP-UP will be back for not one, but TWO days.

Join us for a 𝗙𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗦𝘂𝗺𝗺𝗲𝗿 𝗦𝗶𝗽-&-𝗦𝗵𝗼𝗽 filled with fashion, accessories, décor, gifting, delicious eats, and of course, award-winning Wildekrans wine and bubbly! 🍷✨

What better way to shop for unique gifts and summer wardrobe finds than with a glass in hand, surrounded by vineyards and mountain views? Come experience the sisters. SHOP-UP magic - a celebration of fabulous female-owned brands and the chance to meet the inspiring women behind them.

Skip the malls and busy Hermanus traffic this festive season and support small South African businesses, all while soaking up the charm of beautiful Botrivier. 🌿

👉 Keep an eye on this event for updates on who’ll be joining us and what we have in store - trust us, you don’t want to miss it!
