## About the event

Christmas Market 2026

Join us at Rivergate Guest Farm for our highly anticipated annual December Christmas Market. Gather your family and friends for a beautiful summer day out in the tranquil Stanford countryside.

Discover a curated selection of over 80 amazing local artisans and small businesses showcasing unique gifts, clothing, jewellery, ceramics, and festive decor. It is the perfect opportunity to complete your holiday shopping while supporting local entrepreneurs.

While you shop, stock your pantry with delicious deli treats, artisanal cheeses, and fresh bread. Sip on local wines, craft beers, and boutique gins, or enjoy a freshly brewed coffee or smoothie while soaking in the vibrant farm atmosphere.

Relax on the lawns, say hello to our friendly alpacas and miniature donkeys, and enjoy live music under the summer sun.
