## About the event

Paddakoor Akademie Presents - CHRISTMAS CARNIVAL

Get ready for a magical afternoon filled with Christmas cheer, family fun and festive excitement! 🎅🎁

Join us for a wonderful day of carnival games, delicious food, sweet treats, face painting, jumping castles and so much more! There will be prizes to win, fun for the whole family, and a special visit from Father Christmas himself! Make sure to bring a gift for Father Christmas to hand out!🎅

📍 Venue: Jukskei Hall, Sandbaai

📅 Date: Saturday, 12 December 2026

⏰ Time: 12:00 – 17:00

🎟️ Entrance: R10 per person

Bring your family, invite your friends and come celebrate the most wonderful time of the year with us!

Every bit of support helps Paddakoor Akademie make this event special for our children. ❤️

Let's make Christmas magical, one little smile at a time! 🎄✨
