## About the event

Zolani Mahola + The Feminine Force (featuring Karen Zoid) live at Stanford Hills Sunset Sessions

Zolani Mahola + The Feminine Force (featuring Karen Zoid) | Stanford Hills Sunset Sessions

Join us at Stanford Hills on Tues/15/Dec for the perfect way to start the holidays, as Zolani Mahola and her band "The Feminine Force" welcome a very special guest - Karen Zoid - to the stage.

This special evening of music is a unique celebration of diversity and sisterhood in a setting built for memorable moments. As the sun goes down over the farm, voices, rhythms and stories from different worlds come together in one unforgettable live performance. Expect powerhouse vocals, songs that will move your body and soul, and the kind of electricity that only happens when great artists share a stage.

Details

Date: Tuesday, 15 December 2026

Performance: 18:00 (Gates open @ 15:00)

Venue: Stanford Hills, Stanford, Western Cape

Tickets: Adults R350, Children (5 to 13) R200, children under 5 are free.

Capacity: Tickets are limited, so book early!

Bring a blanket, bring your friends and your family, and come celebrate an evening of music and togetherness.

No outside food and drink allowed, food + drink will be available for sale.
