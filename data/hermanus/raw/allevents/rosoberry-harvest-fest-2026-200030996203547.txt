## About the event

Rosoberry Harvest Fest 2026

A berry good day at the farm 🍓

Berry picking, good food, live music and a whole farm to explore! 🌿

Join us for the second Rosoberry Harvest Fest at Hermanus River Farm — a relaxed, family-friendly celebration of berry season, local food and summer in the Overstrand.

🍓 Your ticket includes berry picking! You’ll receive one full punnet and one empty punnet on arrival, so you can enjoy picking and eating berries throughout the day. Want to take more home? Additional punnets will be available to purchase.

🍨 Come hungry! Enjoy a taste of the Overberg with:

* Hermanus River Farm's grass-fed beef in brisket bowls and espetadas by Agteros Heritage Table and boerie rolls

* Poke bowls by Poke Oukie

* Ice cream by You Scream Ice Cream

* Belgian-style ales from Honingklip Brewery

* Local wine from Gabrielskloof

🎶 Browse fresh farm produce and berry plants from Rosoberry Nursery, relax under the trees to live music by some fantastic artists including Craig Vee, Takura Terry, CJ Bergh, Emma Minke, and Mike Fabricius, and explore our beautiful 2.5 km waterfall walking trail.

Included in your ticket there’s plenty for the kids too, with tractor rides, face painting and lawn games! 🚜

📅 Saturday, 28 November 2026

⏰ 10am–5pm

📍 Hermanus River Farm, on the R44 near Kleinmond https://maps.app.goo.gl/7zU4y5bLFkyNZ34BA?g_st=ac

🎟️ Adults R200 | Children under 12 R70 https://qkt.io/harvestfest26

Bring a picnic blanket and camping chair, settle in and make a day of it - and if you want to make a weekend of it, our campsite will be open with limited sites available. Enquire directly to book a site.
