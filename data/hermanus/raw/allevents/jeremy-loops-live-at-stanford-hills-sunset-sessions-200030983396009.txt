Jeremy Loops Live at Stanford Hills Sunset Sessions

Catch South African live music sensation Jeremy Loops at Stanford Hills Estate on 23 December 2026!

Set among the rolling hills of Stanford, this highly anticipated annual show has become a highlight of Jeremy’s summer tour calendar. Described by Jeremy as “one of the most beautiful live shows” he’s ever played, it’s a firm favourite on the festive season calendar.

Bring your friends and family and join us for an epic day of live music, good food and sunshine.

This show sells out year after year, so get your tickets early! See you there!
