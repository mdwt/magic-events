## About the event

Overberg Toy Run

Toys taken for a ride on the wild side....

About 400 motorcyclists took part in the annual Overberg Toy Run 2025, collecting more than three bakkie‑loads of toys for underprivileged children. Over 100 riders left Hermanus and another group from Gansbaai, meeting in Caledon before finishing at Birkenhead Brewery in Stanford. Bikes decked with teddy bears and Christmas colours rode the Hemel‑en‑Aarde and Akkedisberg routes, and donations were handed to Rotary clubs and community groups for regional distribution.

Mass rides start at 09h00 at Hermanus Whale Coast Mall Mac'Donalds, heading via Hemel-en-Aarde to Caledon, and at 09h00 from the Wimpy in Gansbaai, heading to Bredasdorp. Marshals will be there to guide you! 10h00 start at Caltex (near Casino) in Caledon and 10h00 start at Pavilion Mall, Bredasdorp - with all bikers from Gansbaai and Hermanus - to Stanford (Birkenhead Brewery). Remember to smile en route - there will be photographers capturing you, your pillion and toy-laden bikes!!!
