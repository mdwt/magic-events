# Lemm’s Corner Market Hermanus

## Lemm’s Corner Market

Lemm’s Corner Market is historically the oldest established market in Hermanus. It is right in the Center of town, surrounded by art galleries, restaurants and shops.

### Every Sunday it comes alive with a wonderful vibey atmosphere of music, wine, interesting art, artifacts, articles to buy and mingling locals & tourists.

# Celebrating Joy and Family Fun! Historically still the oldest market in Hermanus!

Every Sunday!
Live Music and 40 stalls to enjoy!

## Lemms Corner Market on Facebook

The Lemms’ Corner Market is a Sunday Market that the entire family can join, with Arts, Crafts, Slow Food and a Band to enjoy and relax.
