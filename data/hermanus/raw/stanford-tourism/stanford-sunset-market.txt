## Stanford Sunset Market

082 667 0619

-34.44011492801233, 19.455456733703613

philippa.murray@gmail.com

Come to this local food and wine market on the historic Market Square on the last Friday evening of each month (September – May). Live music, local wine farms and craft breweries, artisanal foods, and gift stalls mean there is something for everyone. Plenty of seating. Plenty of parking. Fun for all ages.

If the weather has you worried, please call ahead to confirm on 082 667 0619

Or follow us on Facebook or Instagram

https://www.facebook.com/stanfordsunsetmarket/
