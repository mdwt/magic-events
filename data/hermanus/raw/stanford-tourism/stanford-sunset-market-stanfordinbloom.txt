# Stanford Sunset Market

September 18 @ 17:30 - 20:30

- « Indigenous South Africa with Chef Bridget Bartleman: Guineafowl Ravioli – Klein River Cheese Demo Kitchen | R185

- Kids Activities on the Village Green with Eduden Village School »

Please note that for Stanford in Bloom we are thrilled to announce TWO Sunset Markets in September: 18 + 25 September

Stanford Sunset Market is a monthly open air market featuring artisanal food, craft drinks, loaves, pickles, relishes, food trucks, coffee, local produce, plants and gifts that takes place on the central heritage green in the middle of Stanford village under the setting sun. Free access, free parking, all welcome. Loads of lovely people, great vibes, local rhythms and musicians, and a downright old-fashioned good time to be had by all. Last Friday of the month from 6pm.
