About

Join us for a captivating open-air evening with Sterling EQ, South Africa’s all-female crossover ensemble and SAMA winners, as they bring classical music to life with a bold contemporary twist.

Settle into your spot with blankets, cushions or camping chairs and enjoy soaring melodies under the Walker Bay sky at Landskein.
Time: Gates open at 5:00 PM | Concert starts at 7:30 PM
Venue: The Amphitheatre at Landskein, Stanford
Landskein wines, refreshing drinks, wood-fired pizza, popcorn and snacks will be available for purchase.

Please note: No outside food or beverages permitted.
Should adverse weather conditions prevent the event from going ahead, guests will be notified via email.
