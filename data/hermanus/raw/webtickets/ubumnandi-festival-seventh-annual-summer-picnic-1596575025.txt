About

Ubumnandi Festival is a Dance Music Festival started in Hermanus in the Overstrand Region of the Western Cape in South Africa founded by Master Blaq Entertainment (PTY) LTD Trading as Blue Sky South Africa.

This high-profile Festival provides an opportunity for youth in different art codes to showcase their talent and skills for a big crowd of people and supporters from all over the country. The applicable art codes at this present moment are Music, Photography, Videography, Graphic Designs, Entrepreneurship, Drawing, Local Market etc.
