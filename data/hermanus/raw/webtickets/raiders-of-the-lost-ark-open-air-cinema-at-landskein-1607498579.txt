About

Dust off your fedora – Indiana Jones is coming to Landskein! Join the fearless archaeologist on his daring hunt for a legendary relic in Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981), as the Walker Bay sky fills with stars.

Settle into your spot with blankets, cushions or camping chairs and enjoy an unforgettable evening of iconic characters, sharp dialogue and cinematic flair beneath twinkling fairy lights.

Time: Gates open 5:00 PM | Movie starts 7:30 PM
Venue: The Amphitheatre at Landskein, Stanford

Landskein wines, drinks, wood-fired pizza, popcorn and snacks available for purchase. No outside food or beverages permitted. In case of adverse weather, guests will be notified via email.
