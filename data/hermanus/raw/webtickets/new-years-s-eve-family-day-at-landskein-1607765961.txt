About

Spend one last carefree afternoon of the year at Landskein before the countdown begins. Live music by Groove DJ, a jumping castle and water slide make for a joyful family day.
Settle into your spot with blankets, cushions or camping chairs and enjoy an all-you-can-eat braai: lamb chops, boerewors, chicken thighs, assorted salads, freshly baked bread and desserts.
Time: Gates open at 12:00 PM | Groove DJ from 2:00 PM | Ends at 6:00 PM
Venue: Landskein, Stanford
Landskein wines and refreshing drinks will be available for purchase.
Please note: No outside food or beverages permitted.
