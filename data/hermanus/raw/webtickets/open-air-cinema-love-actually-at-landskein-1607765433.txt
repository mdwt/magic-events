About

Join us for a heart-warming open-air cinema night as we screen Richard Curtis’ Love Actually, the much-loved festive classic of interwoven love stories, laughter and Christmas cheer.
Settle into your spot with blankets, cushions or camping chairs and enjoy an unforgettable evening of romance, humour and holiday magic at Landskein.
Time: Gates open at 5:30 PM | Movie starts at 8:00 PM
Venue: The Amphitheatre at Landskein, Stanford
Landskein wines, refreshing drinks, wood-fired pizza, popcorn and snacks will be available for purchase.
Please note: No outside food or beverages permitted.
Should adverse weather conditions prevent the event from going ahead, guests will be notified via emai
