About

Celebrate the magic of Christmas at Carols by Candlelight, as the 90 voices of the Overberg Choir, joined by special guests Dezi Ray & Louna Stofberg, fill the night with beloved festive classics.

Settle into your spot with blankets, cushions or camping chairs and let the choir usher you into the Christmas spirit at Landskein.
Time: Gates open at 5:00 PM | Concert starts at 7:30 PM
Venue: The Amphitheatre at Landskein, Stanford
Landskein wines, refreshing drinks, wood-fired pizza, popcorn and snacks will be available for purchase.

Please note: No outside food or beverages permitted.
