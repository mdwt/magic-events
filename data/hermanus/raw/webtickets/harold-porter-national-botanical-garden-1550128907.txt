About

Harold Porter National Botanical garden, situated in Betty’s Bay, showcases diverse plant species, this tranquil oasis features waterfalls, ponds, and walking trails, explore the garden’s beauty, with flora ranging from fynbos to forest species, and enjoy the serene atmosphere.
