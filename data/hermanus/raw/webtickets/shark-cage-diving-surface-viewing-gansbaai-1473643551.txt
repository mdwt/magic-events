About

Dispel those myths and fears and go Shark Cage Diving Today!

Experience their true magnificence with an up close and personal encounter with sharks. The viewing decks onboard the boat offer spectacular surface viewing with cage diving as optional.

No scuba diving experience is needed. All ages are welcome, young and old.

Sharks you may encounter: Copper Sharks, Sevengill Cow Sharks, and the rare Great White Shark.
Tour Duration: approx. 4 hrs. (depending on shark activity/sea conditions. Excludes 2.5 - 3hr. travel time)

Tour Includes:
Breakfast or lunch on arrival
Shark cage diving & surface viewing
Drinks and snacks on the boat
Use of the dive gear
A warm meal on return to shore

Please remember we are dealing with wildlife and shark sightings can never be guaranteed.

Time slots may vary according to sea/tidal & weather conditions and the number of trips we can run on the day.

Your tour details and exact meeting/collection time will be confirmed the day before.

Don't forget to bring:
Something warm to wear (fleece or a wind-proof jacket)
Comfortable shoes
Swimming trunks/bathing suit (wear underneath your clothes)
Towel
Hat, sunglasses & sunscreen
Camera
Seasickness or motion sickness tablets if appropriate (consult a pharmacy, we are not authorized to dispense any medication)

Bookings close 48 hours before time of tour. For last minute booking inquiries please email us on: info@sharkdiving.co

Or Contact us on: WhatsApp +27 82 403 0333
