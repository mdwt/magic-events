About

This festive season, the Rotary Club of Hermanus invites you to gather under the stars for Hermanus Carols a joyful celebration in our beautiful town.

· Children are invited to meet Father Christmas (don’t forget to bring a present with your child’s name on it!)

·Experience the powerful voices, vibrant energy and rich African rhythms of Hermanus’s Zwelihle Youth Choir in a captivating performance filled with uplifting harmonies.

· Be moved by the Nativity Play, led by Bass Baritone Corniël Calitz and well known Hermanus singers · Sing along with the choir to heartwarming carols under the night sky

· Grab a hearty snack from the local food stalls or bring your own picnic basket and blanket and settle in for an evening of festive cheer.

All funds raised will support Rotary projects, including Early Childhood Development and the Bursary Fund for the Overstrand Learning Hub.

Gates open 18:00 with pre-carols entertainment starting at 18:30, and the Nativity Play, 19:30 – 20:45

Bring your picnic basket with drinks, snacks, and a blanket

Children 6 and below Free
