About

Spend a glorious summer evening surrounded by the spectacular natural beauty of Harold Porter National Botanical Garden in Bettys’ Bay, with award-winning international saxophonist Andrew Young, featuring Tony Drake on keyboards.
Set against the magnificent backdrop of the Kogelberg mountains and indigenous fynbos, this special outdoor picnic concert is the perfect way to enjoy the festive season with family and friends.
From the timeless music of The Beatles and unforgettable movie themes to soul, rock, rhythm & blues and much-loved favourites, Andrew brings his unmistakable sound, exceptional musicianship, infectious energy, warmth and humour to the stage.
Bring your picnic, spread out
