## A gathering of neighbours, long before it was a celebration.

since 2014

Each year, as harvest drew near, the winemakers of the Hemel-en-Aarde came together to taste, to talk and to awaken their senses for the vintage ahead. A ritual shared quietly among the vines.

In 2014 they opened the doors, and the celebration became one of the most anticipated dates on the South African wine calendar. In time, Chardonnay took its place beside Pinot Noir.

Now, the tradition begins a new chapter. Between Heaven and Earth takes this much-loved spirit beyond its origins, bringing a fresh perspective to a celebration rooted in friendship, shared passion and a deep appreciation of wine.

the discovery tasting

### Then the day starts to lift.

keep your glass in hand

Entertainment lifts the mood and transports you, while discussion corners invite you to mingle, listen and be inspired by the people behind the wine. Keep your glass in hand and watch the sky begin to change.

What happens in between is our story to keep, and yours to uncover.

Sunset till late

### As the sun begins to set, the magic of heaven unfolds.

between heaven & earth

The ultimate wine bar pops up in the heart of the Hemel-en-Aarde. Top sommeliers, winemakers and the custodians of our wine farms pour some of South Africa's finest. Fire-cooked food, incredible sounds and entertainment all night.

You’ll leave with a longing for more: more of this extraordinary place, more of its wines and, above all, more of the wonderful people.

## Good to know

everything you need before you arrive

What should I wear?

Dress up, not down. One dress code for the entire day, so come dressed to impress and feeling fabulous from the first pour to the last dance. With lawns underfoot and ancient oaks overhead, leave the stilettos at home and opt for something a little more comfortable.

Where is it?

Nidderdale, in the heart of the Hemel-en-Aarde Valley, just outside Hermanus. The day begins on the lawns beneath the mountain, where the welcome, tasting and lunch unfold. As the afternoon gives way to evening, the celebration moves beneath the canopy of the old oak trees. Full directions will be shared with your ticket.

Is there a break during the day?

No. The celebration runs from 11am until late, flowing seamlessly from one moment to the next. No breaks, no pauses and certainly no reason to leave. The In-Between is there to bridge the afternoon into the evening, with a space to freshen up and get ready for whatever the night brings.

How do I get home safely?

Sip, savour, and leave the driving to someone else. Please don’t drink and drive. Book one of our trusted local shuttle partners to get you to Nidderdale and safely home after the last glass under the stars.

- Tuk-Taxi Hermanus

- Hermanus Shuttle

- Hermanus Dassie

- Percy’s Tours

Uber can be unreliable in Hermanus, especially later in the evening, so we recommend booking your shuttle ahead of time.

Can I buy tickets for my friends?

Absolutely. Simply select the number of places you require and add each guest’s name at checkout. These names will be printed on your individual table cards and tasting notes, so please take a moment to double-check the spelling. We want every detail to be just right.
