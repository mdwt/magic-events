Christmas Eve Dinner at Moody Lagoon Restaurant, near Hermanus

There is something truly special about Christmas Eve.

The gifts are wrapped, the preparations are complete, and for one evening, family and friends come together to slow down, reconnect, and celebrate the moments that matter most.

Join us on Thursday, 24 December 2026, for an elegant Christmas Eve Dinner at Moody Lagoon Restaurant. Set on the water's edge at Benguela Cove Wine Estate, this exclusive festive dining experience combines exceptional cuisine, award-winning wine, and breathtaking sunset views across the lagoon. Dinner service begins from 18:00.

As daylight fades and the estate glows with festive warmth, settle in for an evening of fine food, good company, and memorable conversation. Whether you're beginning a holiday getaway, hosting loved ones from near or far, or creating a new festive tradition, Christmas Eve at Benguela Cove promises an unforgettable start to Christmas celebrations.

The highlight of the evening is a specially curated multi-course festive dinner at Moody Lagoon Restaurant, thoughtfully prepared by our Head Chef using seasonal, locally sourced ingredients.

A Christmas Eve Feast to Remember

Leave the cooking to us and enjoy a relaxed evening where every detail has been taken care of.

-
To Start: A sharing herb-infused milk bun wreath with a warm melted camembert centre, accompanied by selected cold meats, cocktail tomatoes, and artisanal preserves.

-
Main Course: Choose between honey-glazed gammon, traditional stuffed chicken with rich gravy, or smoked roast beef finished with num-num berry and red wine jus. Served alongside festive favourites including crispy Pommes Anna with cheese sauce, mature cheddar gem squash, sweet corn, and green beans with shaved Parmesan.

-
Dessert: Complete the evening with Amarula Malva pudding served with Rooibos crème anglaise, or a decadent summer trifle layered with seasonal berries, dark chocolate sauce, and chocolate sponge

VIEW CHRISTMAS MENU | VIEW KIDS MENU

Our children's menu ensures younger guests can enjoy a festive meal of their own while parents relax and savour the occasion. With giant courtyard games, sprawling lawns and gorgeous play areas, this is an ideal family-friendly dining experience.

Reserve Your Christmas Eve Table

This exclusive Christmas Eve dining experience is available for one night only, with limited seating available.

Book online now or contact us on 087 357 0637 or info@benguelacove.co.za.

A 50% deposit is required to secure your reservation.

Gourmet Christmas Picnics This December

Looking for a more relaxed way to celebrate the festive season? Enjoy a Gourmet Christmas Picnic surrounded by spectacular lagoon views and summer sunshine at Benguela Cove.

Available Wednesday to Sunday throughout December | 11:00-16:00

- Gourmet picnic baskets filled with seasonal favourites

- Perfect for families, friends and holidaymakers

- Booking essential at least 24 hours in advance

View the Picnic Menu

View Kids Picnic Menu

Spend the Day Exploring the Estate

Arrive early and make the most of your visit before dinner. At Benguela Cove, guests can enjoy a range of experiences designed to make the festive season even more memorable.

-
Wine Experiences: Discover our award-winning cool-climate wines through guided tastings, food pairings, cellar tours, and vineyard safaris.

-
Family Activities: Challenge the family to pirate-themed adventure golf, explore the Splash Waterpark, and enjoy outdoor lawn games and play areas.

-
Festive Shopping: Browse our gift shop for estate wines, artisanal products, luxury accessories, children's gifts, and seasonal treats.

Wake Up at Benguela Cove on Christmas Morning

Make Christmas Eve even more magical by staying overnight in one of our luxury waterfront villas.

Enjoy dinner knowing there's no journey home afterwards. Wander beneath the stars, relax beside your private pool or wood-fired hot tub, and wake up on Christmas morning surrounded by the beauty of the estate.

Residences such as Swallows Rest accommodate up to ten guests across five en-suite bedrooms and offer premium amenities including complete inverter backup power, spacious entertainment areas, and spectacular waterfront views.

For many guests, it's the most relaxing and memorable way to experience Christmas at Benguela Cove.
