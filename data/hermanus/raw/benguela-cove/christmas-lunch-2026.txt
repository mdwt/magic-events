Christmas Day Magic at Moody Lagoon Restaurant

Secure your table now for Christmas Lunch at Moody Lagoon restaurant on Friday, 25 December 2026. Celebrate the holiday season at Benguela Cove Wine Estate for a vibrant, sun-drenched South African summer Christmas. Gather your family to enjoy cool Atlantic breezes, bright blue skies, and an unforgettable festive atmosphere right on the water’s edge.

Join us for brunch on 25 December, served 09:30-11:30 or a leisurely Christmas Picnic, available this December Wednesday-Sunday from 11:00-16:00 (book 24 hours in advance).

The highlight of the day is a magnificent Christmas lunch at Moody Lagoon restaurant starting from 12:00pm. Our Head Chef has designed a spectacular multi-course summer feast using seasonal, locally sourced ingredients.

Christmas Lunch Menu at Benguela Cove

Download the Christmas Lunch Menu | Kids Menu | Christmas Picnic Menu

-
To Start: A sharing herb-infused milk bun wreath with a warm, melted camembert centre, adorned with selected cold meats, cocktail tomatoes, and artisanal preserves.

- The Main Feast: Your choice of honey-glazed gammon, traditional stuffed chicken with rich gravy, or smoked roast beef completed with a local num-num berry and red wine jus. All mains include classic holiday sides: crispy Pommes Anna with cheese sauce, sweet corn and mature cheddar gem squash halves, and green beans with shaved parmesan.

-
Dessert: Conclude with an indulgent Amarula Malva pudding with Rooibos crème anglaise, or a decadent summer trifle layered with fresh seasonal berries, dark chocolate sauce, and chocolate sponge.

Kid's Christmas Menu

Our fussy-eater approved children's menu ensures the entire family enjoys a memorable holiday feast, with fun things to do for guests of all ages.

Download the Kids Christmas Menu.

Book Your Table for Christmas Lunch at Benguela Cove

Book online now, or contact us on 087 357 0637 or info@benguelacove.co.za

Booking and payment of a 50% deposit is essential to secure your place for this unforgettable Christmas celebration.

Gourmet Christmas Picnics This December

Looking for a more relaxed way to celebrate the festive season? Enjoy a Gourmet Christmas Picnic surrounded by spectacular lagoon views and summer sunshine at Benguela Cove.

Available Wednesday to Sunday throughout December | 11:00-16:00

- Gourmet picnic baskets filled with seasonal favourites

- Perfect for families, friends and holidaymakers

- Booking essential at least 24 hours in advance

View the Picnic Menu

View Kids Picnic Menu

Book your Christmas Picnic via 087 357 0637 or info@benguelacove.co.za

Things to do on Christmas Day: Festive Fun & Activities at the Estate

Turn your Christmas lunch into a full day of memories. Our world-class wine estate offers experiences for guests of all ages before or after your meal:

-
Family Adventures: Challenge the family to 18 holes of pirate-themed adventure golf, enjoy the Splash waterpark, giant courtyard games, and gorgeous play areas on sprawling lawns.

-
Wine & Vineyards: Discover our award-winning cool-climate wines through expert-led wine tasting and food pairing experiences, cellar tours, and custom wine-blending workshops. Our vineyard safari is a bucket-list experience, where you can taste the wines in the vineyards.

-
Holiday Shopping: Visit the gift shop to explore artisanal treats, accessories, branded cheese boards, luxury decanters, estate-grown Extra Virgin olive oil, pop-art sculptures, and children's toys.

Make It a Summer Getaway

Extend the festive celebrations by booking a luxury stay in one of our waterfront villas. Residences like Swallows Rest sleep up to ten guests across five en-suite bedrooms and feature a private swimming pool, wood-fired hot tub, and complete inverter backup power to keep your holiday uninterrupted. It serves as the ultimate destination to relax and recharge with your loved ones this December.
