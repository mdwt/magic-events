## That ’80s Show: 80s Tribute Concert near Hermanus

That ’80s Show brings the music of the 1980s to Benguela Cove Lagoon Wine Estate in Hermanus. Mike McCullagh and The Harmonix perform an 80s tribute concert covering Queen, Tina Turner, Journey, Madonna and more, with costume changes and an encore. Choose Saturday 12 December 2026 or Saturday 27 February 2027. Book two tickets with a picnic basket and a bottle of wine for R1 550, or tickets on their own for R350.

## At a glance

-
Dates: Saturday, 12 December 2026 | Saturday, 27 February 2027

-
Doors open: 18:30

-
Show starts: 19:30

-
Wine Pairing Basket for 2 + Entry: R1 550 (two tickets, a picnic for two and a bottle of wine)

-
General Admission: R350 per person

-

-

-
Dress code: 80s, dressing up encouraged

-
Venue: Benguela Cove Lagoon Wine Estate, Walker Bay, 15 km (12 minutes) from Hermanus town (get directions)

-
Chauffeurs and shuttle services to and from the estate are also available.

###

What happens at the 80s tribute concert?

Expect a party. Mike McCullagh and The Harmonix recreate the sound and look of 80s icons, with vocal impressions of Freddie Mercury and Tina Turner and songs from Journey, Queen and Madonna. Costume changes run through the night, and the show closes with an encore.

Sing along, get up and dance, and come dressed for it. Neon, denim, big hair and shoulder pads all fit.

## Picnics and platters at the show

The easiest way to eat at the show is the Wine Pairing Basket for 2 + Entry. One booking secures two tickets and your picnic, ready at your table when doors open at 18:30.

-
Wine Pairing Basket (in the R1 550 package). Serves two. Ciabatta, cheese straws, olives, butter, preserve, cous cous salad, Greek salad, dry wors, beef biltong, two cheeses, two speciality cold meats, cheesecake, a chocolate brownie and caramel popcorn, with a bottle of Lighthouse Moody Lagoon Red, Sauvignon Blanc, Cuvée Brut or Cuvée Rosé. The basket comes with ceramic plates, metal cutlery and glass wine glasses.

-
Benguela Cove Cellar Door Platter. R600, serves two to three. Four artisanal charcuterie cuts, four handcrafted cheeses, home-made preserves, fresh fruit, breads and accompaniments. Ask our ambassadors about a vegetarian option.

PICNIC MENU • TASTING ROOM PLATTER MENU

Pre-book picnics and platters at least 48 hours before the show. For pre-orders and enquiries, contact our reservations team Mon-Fri, 08:00-17:00 on 087 357 0637 or via info@benguelacove.co.za.

##

Lunch or dinner before the show

Make a day of it with lunch at Moody Lagoon Restaurant from the summer à la carte menu, with line fish, slow-cooked lamb shank and oysters shucked to order.

BOOK YOUR TABLE • À LA CARTE LUNCH MENU

For dinner, book Fire on the Lagoon at Moody Lagoon Restaurant, an open-fire menu cooked over rooikrans and vine prunings, from 17:30. Choose from Karoo lamb shoulder smoked for twelve hours, beef rump picanha or a whole Struisbaai yellowtail for four. The last seating is at 19:00. Moody Lagoon is in a different part of the estate from the show, so finish your meal before the show starts at 19:30.

## Make an evening of it

Arrive early and start in the Tasting Room, open until 20:00 on Saturdays. The Estate Tasting pours five estate wines for R220 per person.

Stay over in one of the Waterfront Villas and walk back after the encore. The estate sits 15 km from Hermanus town and about 1 hour 10 minutes from Cape Town.

## That ’80s Show FAQs

#### What time should I arrive?

Doors open at 18:30 and the show starts at 19:30. If you’re having dinner at Fire on the Lagoon, book a seating by 19:00 and allow time to get to the show venue before the first song.

#### Will I have a seat?

Yes. The show has unreserved tables and chairs, first come, first served. Arrive when doors open at 18:30 to choose your spot. Picnics and platters you’ve pre-booked come to your table.

#### What should I wear?

Dress in 80s style: neon, denim, big hair, shoulder pads or a full costume. Walker Bay evenings cool down after sunset, so bring a light jacket.

#### Can I book a table for a group?

Absolutely, email info@benguelacove.co.za. directly and we can discuss your requirements. For a birthday or work night, book General Admission tickets together and pre-order Cellar Door Platters to share. Each platter serves two to three people. Couples can book the Wine Pairing Basket for 2 + Entry.

#### Is there accommodation on the estate?

Yes. The Waterfront Villas are on the estate, so you can walk back to your villa after the show. Check dates and availability on the Waterfront Villas page.

#### How far is Benguela Cove from Cape Town?

Benguela Cove is 95 km from Cape Town CBD, about 1 hour 10 minutes by car, and 88 km (about 1 hour) from Cape Town International Airport. Hermanus town is 15 km away, roughly 12 minutes’ drive.
