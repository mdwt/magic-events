## Join the Benguela Cove parkrun Every Saturday Morning in Hermanus

Join us every Saturday morning at 8 AM for the Benguela Cove parkrun 5km. This is the ultimate way to start your weekend surrounded by breathtaking vineyards and stunning views. Please note the Park Run is weather-dependent.

#### When can you add Benguela Cove to your parkrun profile?

Every Saturday.

#### What does it cost to join in?

Nothing - it's free!

#### Enjoy a Fun, Friendly, and Free Weekly 5km Run or Walk

This community event welcomes walkers, joggers, runners, volunteers, spectators, and leashed furry friends. Bring the whole family to enjoy exercise, fresh air, and natural beauty.

#### Reward Yourself Afterwards with a Delicious Breakfast in Hermanus

Once you've worked up an appetite, reward yourself with our delicious Breakfast Special served at Blackbeard's Diner starting at 8:30 AM.

OR

Enjoy a satisfying brunch in Hermanus at Moody Lagoon restaurant served until 11:30 AM.

Get Ready for the Perfect Blend of Nature, Fitness, and Brunch Dining!
