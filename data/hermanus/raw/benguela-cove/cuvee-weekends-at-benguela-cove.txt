Cuvée Weekends at Benguela Cove

Book Now

## Bottomless Bubbly Brunch Special Offer

Two Hours. Freely Poured. Beautifully Unhurried.

Join us at Moody Lagoon Restaurant every Saturday, Sunday, and Public Holiday from 11:00 to 13:00 for Cuvée Weekends, a relaxed brunch experience overlooking the tranquil lagoon, mountains, and the Atlantic Ocean beyond.

Settle in with a delicious brunch and enjoy two hours of freely poured Cuvée 58 Brut NV Cap Classique, crafted from grapes grown right here on the estate. With vineyard views, exceptional food, and endless sparkling wine, it's the perfect way to start your weekend.

## Brunch Special Offer Details

Every Saturday, Sunday & Public Holiday
11:00 - 13:00

Price: R295 per person. Includes two hours of freely poured Cuvée 58 Brut NV Cap Classique when ordered with any brunch or lunch dish. Brunch and lunch dishes not included.

Wine Served: Cuvée 58 Brut NV Cap Classique. Served in 125ml pours throughout the experience.

Venue: Moody Lagoon Restaurant, Benguela Cove Wine Estate, R43, Hermanus. Approximately 25 minutes from central Hermanus and 90 minutes from Cape Town.

### What Makes Cuvée Weekends Special?

Estate-Grown Excellence

The grapes used to craft your Cap Classique are grown on the surrounding slopes visible from your table, creating a true vineyard-to-glass experience.

One Fixed Price

Enjoy unlimited refills throughout your two-hour experience for one set price, allowing you to relax and savour the moment.

Reserved Brunch Seating

With dedicated arrival times of 11:00 and 11:30, every table enjoys ample time to relax before the experience concludes at 13:00.

## Make a Day of It

A visit to Benguela Cove offers far more than brunch. Extend your stay and discover everything the estate has to offer.

-
Wine Tasting & Cellar Experiences: Explore our award-winning cool-climate wines through guided tastings and cellar experiences located adjacent to the restaurant.

-
Pirate Adventure Golf: Challenge family and friends on our popular 18-hole pirate-themed mini golf course.

-
Splash Pad: Relax with a glass of wine while younger guests enjoy the outdoor splash area.

-
Luxury Waterfront Villas: Turn brunch into a weekend getaway with a stay in one of Benguela Cove's luxury waterfront villas, located on the estate.

### Frequently Asked Questions

How does the two-hour experience work?

Your two-hour Cuvée Weekend begins from the moment your first glass of Cuvée 58 Brut NV is poured at your table.

Is booking required?

Yes. Advance booking is essential through DinePlan or the Benguela Cove reservations team 087 357 0637 • info@benguelacove.co.za. Seating is available at fixed arrival times of 11:00 and 11:30.

What is the cancellation policy?

We kindly request a minimum of 24 hours' notice for cancellations or changes to guest numbers.

Can children attend?

Absolutely. Moody Lagoon welcomes families, and children can enjoy a variety of on-estate activities, including Pirate Adventure Golf and the Splash Pad.

Can non-drinkers join the booking?

Yes. Non-drinking guests are welcome and may order from the brunch menu.
