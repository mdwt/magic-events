Andrew Young Live Concert

Book Now

#### One of the world's great saxophonists. One spectacular summer afternoon.

Saturday, 9 January 2027 | Doors 16:30 | Show 17:30

He’s back.

After captivating Benguela Cove audiences before, internationally acclaimed saxophonist Andrew Young returns to the lagoon for another unforgettable afternoon of live music, soul, rhythm and blues, rock and timeless favourites.

This is not your standard saxophone concert.

Andrew Young has been described by Saxophone Journal USA as “one of the most complete saxophone players in the world today”, with the P.E. Express calling him one of the most prolific saxophonists ever to perform in South Africa.

Expect Beatles classics, iconic movie themes, soulful favourites and songs you know by heart, transformed by Andrew's extraordinary musicianship, energy and unmistakable style. He has performed everywhere from Europe and Asia to Africa and the Caribbean, and now he is bringing that global musical journey back to Benguela Cove.

## Come for Andrew. Stay for the whole experience.

Doors open at 16:30, with the show starting at 17:30, Benguela Cove's award-winning wines in hand and the lagoon as a backdrop.

Make a full afternoon of it. Moody Lagoon Restaurant serves à la carte lunch from 12:00 to 17:00 on the day, and gourmet picnic baskets, with a wine pairing option, are available on the lawns from 11:00 to 16:00. Browse the full dining options, including Moody Lagoon and the estate's picnics.
