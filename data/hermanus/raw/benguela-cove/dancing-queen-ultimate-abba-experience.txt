## Dancing Queen: The Ultimate ABBA Experience. Live Music. Dining. Disco.

Last year's show sold out. Now Dancing Queen returns for two nights.

Get ready for a night of ABBA, glitter, big choruses and dancing under the stars at Benguela Cove.

Mike & The Harmonix bring their full live ABBA tribute to the estate, complete with costume, staging and all the songs you came to hear. The courtyard becomes your own little Greek island for the night, with the lagoon and mountains setting the scene.

And when the show ends, the night doesn't.

The disco kicks in under the stars, right on the edge of the lagoon.

Saturday, 30 January 2027

Doors open 16:30, band starts at 17:30. Dancing until late.

### ABBA Tribute Tickets

-
Adults: R500

-
Children (under 17): R325

-
Family bundle: R1,450 for 2 adults + 2 children under 17

If you're coming from Hermanus or Cape Town, make a night of it. Eat, drink, dance, stay, and wake up somewhere beautiful.

### Make a night of it

#### Start with a picnic

Arrive early, settle in and watch the light change across Walker Bay with a gourmet picnic basket.

Order ahead and collect when you arrive, then find your spot on the lawns or in the courtyard. Wine, good food, ABBA and a sunset. That's your evening sorted.

- Download the picnic menu

-
Classic basket: R800

-
Wine pairing basket: R950, including a bottle from our Lighthouse Collection

-
Kids' basket: R250

Each adult basket serves two.

Picnics must be booked at least 24 hours in advance on 087 357 0637 or info@benguelacove.co.za.

Order when you book your tickets. Baskets are limited.

### Or make a day of it at Moody Lagoon

#### Prefer a proper lunch before the music starts?

Settle in at Moody Lagoon for an à la carte lunch overlooking the lagoon and Overberg mountains, then wander across to the courtyard when the doors open at 16:30.

It's a very good way to start the night.

### Stay for the encore

#### Why drive home when you can simply walk home?

Benguela Cove has 13 waterfront villas, sleeping 6 to 20 guests, each with a private pool and braai. Dance until late, wander back to your villa, pour one last glass of wine and wake up beside the lagoon.

No designated driver. No late-night road back to Hermanus or Cape Town.

Just ABBA, wine, water and a very slow morning.

Villas book out well ahead of event weekends.
Once you've booked your tickets check availability here or enquire at stay@benguelacove.co.za.
