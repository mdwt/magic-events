## The Blending Table, Make Your Own Wine at Benguela Cove

#### Every guest leaves with a bottle they made themselves.

Once a month, Winemaker opens the cellar to 20 guests for Benguela Cove's Blending Table. You will taste four to six component wines from the current vintage, blend your own final wine at the table using precision pourers and tasting notes, then watch the winemaker reveal his own blend and judge the results. Every guest leaves with a labelled bottle of their own creation.

#### Sunday, 29 November & Sunday, 27 December 2026, 11:00, 2.5 hours.

#### R895 per person, including wines and bottling. 20 Seats only.

Book online now, or contact us on 087 357 0637 or info@benguelacove.co.za.
Wine club members receive access 48 hours before general release.
