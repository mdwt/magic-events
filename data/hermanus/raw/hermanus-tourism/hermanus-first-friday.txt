## Hermanus First Friday

Art in Hermanus is one of the jewels in the crown of our town along with wine, food, adventure and hospitality.

The wealth of unique art in our 26 galleries can be experienced by visitors on any day of the year but every first Friday of the month art galleries remain open until 8pm for a unique experience to “ArtWalk our Town”
Stroll through the village, and enjoy the impressive range of fine art, sculpture, ceramics, photography and jewellery on display.

Collect your ArtWalk map from any of the 26 galleries!
